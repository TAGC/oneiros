# Oneiros: Simplified Chinese names (adopted)

These are suggestions for David to approve; nothing here is final.

**Sources.** The starting point is the two Chinese guides David translated:
- **guide (new)**: "Oneiros：游戏概念简介", 21 Nov 2026, 3 pages.
- **guide (old)**: "Oneiros：游戏构想", an older and longer guide, 11 pages. Some of what it names has since been redesigned.

Every term either guide covers keeps the guide's word. Where the two disagree, or where the old guide names something that has since been redesigned, the row says so and the term is also listed under section 3. A term is marked **proposed** only where neither guide has a word for it. "guide (new, old)" means both guides agree.

Other sources: the legacy vision doc (since migrated into the wiki), the Glossary and sheet tabs, migration map sections 1, 2 and 7, and the pages under `src/content/wiki/`.

## 1. Conventions

### Capitalised terms (Chinese has no capitals)
Several guide terms are ordinary words: 平静 (Calm), 注意 (Attention), 静止 (Stillness), 观察 (Inspect), 本性 (grain), 种类 (kind), 房间, 光环. The marking scheme therefore matters more than it would with coined terms.
1. **Fixed form.** Each term has exactly one Chinese form. Never use a synonym for it, and never use the term's own word in its ordinary sense. For the ordinary sense, use a different everyday word:

   | Term | Ordinary sense |
   | --- | --- |
   | 平静 (Calm) | 安静, 镇定 |
   | 注意 (Attention) | 注意力, 留意 |
   | 静止 (Stillness) | 不动 |
   | 观察 (Inspect) | 看, 查看 |
   | 本性 (grain) | 天性 |
| 引导 (Channelling) | 指引 |
   | 种类 (kind) | 类别 |
   | 念头 (Notion) | 想法 |
   | 确信 (Certainty) | 深信 |

   This is how Chinese does what capitals do in English.
2. **First mention.** Follow both guides: bold the term and give the English in parentheses, e.g. **清醒者**(Lucid). After that, write it plain.
3. **Optional 「」 brackets.** Mainland Chinese writes quotations with “”, so the corner brackets 「」 are free to mark terms. Use them sparingly, only where a sentence could read the term as an ordinary word, e.g. 「平静」靠近奇景时会转为「静止」. Do not use them as a blanket style. In the game UI, states may appear in 【】, which is a common Chinese game convention.
4. **Single characters** (emotion and adverb tokens such as 恨, 远, 稳) appear only in spell chains and readouts, never as wiki aliases.

### Greek names
The policy follows how the guides already handle Greek names:
- **Established Chinese renderings stay.** 俄刻阿诺斯 (Oceanus), 摩涅莫绪涅 (Mnemosyne) and 勒忒 (Lethe) are in the old guide.
- **Philosophical terms take their meaning.** Apeiron is 无定 in the new guide; the old guide had 无限质. Metaxy becomes 居间 (proposed: the standard Platonic rendering). Koinos kosmos and idios kosmos become 共同世界 and 私人世界 (proposed), echoing the Chinese translation of Heraclitus fragment B89.
- **Places in the house take their meaning.** Temenos is 圣域 (both guides). For Megaron, see section 3.
- **Coins and liquid units are transliterated.** The guides give 奥波勒斯 (obol), and 库阿托斯, 科堤勒, 科乌斯 and 安佛拉 (kyathos, kotyle, chous, amphora). For pithos, see section 3.
- **The game's name stays in Latin.** "Oneiros" is never transliterated (both guides).

### Pairs, plurals and measure words
- **Opposed pairs** are written X与Y, as both guides do: 喜悦与悲伤, 嫉妒与善意.
- **Plurals.** Chinese has none, so every plural alias (Megara, euripi, kyathoi, eidola, Beads...) disappears. Aliases should be unique strings, and no single character should be an alias.
- **Measure words.** Fixing these keeps prose consistent: 一段记忆, 一颗珠子, 一枚奥波勒斯, 一条信念, 一则沉吟, 一道传送门.

### Site name and area names
- **Site name:** **Oneiros 维基**. "Oneiros" stays in Latin, as in both guide titles. The alternative is Oneiros 百科; Chinese fan wikis use both.
- **Area names** (UI-facing):

  | English | 简体中文 | English | 简体中文 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Beliefs | 信念 | Library | 藏书室 |
  | Body | 身体与形态 | Lore | 典故 |
  | Character | 角色 | Lucidity | 清明 |
  | Dreams | 你的梦 | Magic | 魔法 |
  | Economy | 经济 | Memory | 记忆 |
  | Emotion | 情感 | Philosophy | 哲学 |
  | Items | 物品 | Rest | 休息 |
  | Temenos | 圣域 | World | 世界 |

  Dreams is 你的梦, the old guide's section title. Lore is 典故 (proposed); 传说 is the alternative, but the old guide uses 传说 for "legends".

- **Page status** (a wiki property, not a game concept): **状态**. The four stages reuse the belief stage terms: Notion 念头, Tenet 信条, Certainty 确信, Dogma 教条. 状态 also appears inside 凝聚度状态; the context keeps the two apart.
- **Canon audit** (a wiki process, the `canon-audit` skill): **正典审核**.
- **The vision** (the designer's original design document, which the wiki migrated and still cites as a source; not a game term): **愿景** (proposed). Older Chinese pages and issues write it "Vision" in Latin letters; new text uses 愿景.
- **Astrolabe** (a wiki tool, the star map of every question; not a game term): **星盘** (proposed; the Chinese name is still open). Its question statuses: frontier **前沿**, blocked **受阻**, decided **已定**, ruled out **不采纳**, undermined **前提动摇**. Its roles: central **核心**, peripheral **边缘**, independent **独立**. Open question stays **待定问题** and conflict **冲突**.
- **Constellation** (wiki tooling, a group of open questions settled in one session; not a game concept): **星座**. Its two scales: **规模** (size): 小 / 中 / 大 (small / medium / large); **精力** (energy): 低 / 中 / 高 (low / medium / high). Proposed.
- **The Astrolabe's Dial** (the constellations view, a brass dial; wiki tooling, not game terms): the view is the Dial, **表盘**. Its parts: sector **扇区** (one per area), ring **圆环** (one per energy), hub **中心**, rim **外缘**, mark **标记** (one per constellation), notch **刻痕** (one per question round a mark). A constellation's GitHub milestone is its **里程碑**. Proposed.
- **The Astrolabe's cluster choice** (how the night sky and the tiers gather their stars; wiki tooling, not game terms): cluster by **聚集方式**, by Areas **领域** or by Constellations **星座**. The cluster of stars in no constellation is **不在任何星座中**. Proposed.
- **The Astrolabe's Show settled switch** (shows every settled question, not only those linked to an open one; wiki tooling): **显示已定问题**, reusing 已定 as in 已定之地. Proposed.
- **Settled blocker** (a blocker that is decided or ruled out, so the question it blocks can reach the frontier; wiki tooling): **已定下**; a blocker that is not settled (open, undermined or unknown) is **尚未定下**, as in the Astrolabe's frontier and blocked help. The Astrolabe's threads take their kinds from it. Proposed.

## 3. Hardest calls (my pick in bold)

Superseded old-guide terms (do not reuse): the **睡眠** (Sleep) source and tracked value; the Coherence-as-gap model (凝聚度 is kept as a name only); status words 昏沉 and 暗流; ladder rungs 深层房间 and 浮现-as-a-tier; 杂物 (clutter); 法则 (beliefs as "laws"); 磨损与燃尽; the old meaning of 信物; 镜子 (sheet-only).

1. **Mood: 心境 (old guide) vs 情绪 (new guide).** Pick **心境**. In everyday Chinese 情绪 is close to 情感 (emotion), which would blur mood (the volume of all eight at once) with emotion (one of them). The old guide used 心境 consistently.
2. **Grief: 悲伤 (new) vs 哀伤 (old, including its intensity table).** Pick **悲伤** (newer), and change the table's 哀伤 and 沉重的哀伤 to match. 哀伤 is the fallback if David prefers its register.
3. **Intensity level 2: long (old guide, 深深的憎恨) vs compact (new guide, 深恨).** Pick **compact** for every emotion, so that readouts stay short. Levels 1 and 3 to 5 stay as the old table. Hope 3 and 4 change from 信念 to **信仰 / 炽热的信仰**, because 信念 is now Belief.
4. **The Dreamer: 做梦者 (new) vs 梦者 (old).** Pick **做梦者**, the newer guide's word. 梦者 is shorter and works in titles if David prefers it.
5. **Apeiron: 无定 (new) vs 无限质 (old).** Pick **无定**. It is newer, it is the established rendering, and 无限 wrongly suggests "infinite".
6. **Obol: 奥波勒斯 (new) vs 奥波拉斯 (old).** Pick **奥波勒斯**, the standard rendering of obolus.
7. **Vivid and Lurid: 鲜梦 (new) vs 鲜明期 / 骇明期 (old).** Pick **鲜梦 / 骇梦**.
8. **Room tier vs ordinary room.** The guide's 房间 is an ordinary word, and the vision and migration map require the tier to be distinct. Pick **独室**, shortened from the new guide's "独立的房间". Megaron becomes **正殿**: the new guide's "承重的大厅" would clash with 中央大厅 (the centre), and 梅加隆 is the transliteration alternative.
9. **Raw: 原始 vs 原生魔法 (both in the old guide).** Pick **原始魔法** (source label 原始). "Raw" means unworked, and 原生 reads as "native".
10. **Unmaking 消解 vs lucidity's "消解" (new guide).** Pick **消解 for Unmaking only**. Lucidity's dissolving becomes **溶解** in prose.
11. **Strength vs Intensity.** The guide fixes Intensity as **强度**, so Strength needs another word. Pick **力度**. 效力 is the alternative; avoid 实力, which the new guide uses for "power".
12. **Extraction 拾忆 (old) vs Gleaning.** 拾 ("pick up") fits gleaning better, but the old guide gave 拾忆 to extraction verbatim. Pick **拾忆 = extraction, 采忆 = gleaning**. If David prefers, swap them: 拾忆 for gleaning, 摄取 for extraction.
13. **Rumour 谣言 (new) vs 传闻 (old).** Pick **谣言**. This freed **传闻** for the bottom belief stage while it was called Hearsay. Since that stage became Notion (念头), 传闻 is unused.
14. **Calm 平静 and Attention 注意 are everyday words.** Keep them, as the guides do, and apply convention 1 strictly: for the ordinary senses, write 安静 / 镇定 and 注意力 / 留意.
15. **Material 材料 (old guide).** Keep **材料**, though it clashes with crafting "materials", as the English does. 质料 is the philosophical alternative if the clash bites.

## 4. Distinctions Chinese forces, or erases

- **Lucid vs lucidity.** English uses one root; the guides use **清醒者** and **清明**. It works, but say on the Lucid page that 清醒 and 清明 are the same idea. Also fix the old guide's 清醒梦 ("waking dream") in Vertical layers.
- **Memory.** The item (记忆), Memory magic (记忆魔法, label 记忆), and memory light (记忆之光) are clean in Chinese. The only clash left is the bare source label 记忆 in tables, which needs a column header such as 来源.
- **Deep.** The phase is 深眠 and the Dreamer's deep is 深渊, so Chinese separates them, as the migration map asks. "The deep layers come closer" becomes 深层. The 深 in 深恨 and 深深地 (Hate) is unrelated, as in English.
- **Light.** The phase is 浅眠, the kind is 光, memory light is 记忆之光, and lit areas are 明处. These are all distinct in Chinese.
- **Crack.** Chinese has to choose a verb. A **room** 开裂 (cracks, a warning that can mend); a **memory** or **bead** 碎裂 (shatters and spills).
- **Dun.** 枯 is used only for an empty bead. The Glossary's scar sense ("drained to dun") is dropped, as the migration map asks.
- **Calm vs calm, Stillness vs still.** In Chinese the term and the ordinary word are the same characters, so convention 1 must carry the distinction.
- **Wonder.** 惊奇 (the state), 惊奇记忆 (wonder memory) and 奇景 (marvel) are distinct.
- **Core.** The guides use 内核 both for the core layer and for a Reverie's unchangeable core, exactly as English does with "core". Keep it, but never make bare 内核 an alias. Alternatively, call the Reverie's core 原核.
- **Anchored.** The status word 稳固 and the anchored place (锚定地点) are separated in Chinese, though the same English word links them. Accept that.
- **Good will.** The Chinese 善意 drops the false link to "will" (意志).
- **Mercy vs Mercifulness.** Settled as Mercy (22 Nov 2026). Both English names are 仁慈 in Chinese, so the Chinese text did not change.
- **Hold vs Conviction.** These are 扎根度 and 信念度. Hold shares 扎根 with the ladder (扎根阶梯), which is apt.
- **Echo, Refrain, Resonance.** These are 回响, 萦回 and 共鸣: three sound metaphors, all distinct. Do not use 回响 for anything else.
- **Illusion, Eidolon, Guise, persona.** These are 幻象, 异影, 扮相 and 外相: all distinct.
- **Planting vs Inception.** These are 种下 (into a place) and 植入 (into a mind). Both mean "plant"; keep the forms fixed.
- **Insight vs Inspirations.** 灵感 and 灵感来源 share 灵感. This is minor; 顿悟 removes the overlap if wanted.
- **Faith (Hope 3) vs belief.** English "Faith" sits close to belief. In Chinese 信仰 is close to 信念 / 信条 but is not one of the belief terms. Flag it, and accept it.
- **Emotion names vs level-1 words.** These are the same word in both languages (喜悦 / Joy), except Hate: 恨 and 憎恨, mirroring Hate and Hatred.
