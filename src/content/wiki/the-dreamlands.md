**The Dreamlands** are the dream of the [[Dreamer]]: the one shared world all players inhabit, each in their own copy. In lore it is the _koinos kosmos_ (see [[Greek names]]). Its counterpart is a will's private [[inner world]].

Everything in the Dreamlands is a will in motion: creatures, fire, water, doors, even stone (see [[Will in motion]]). The name comes from Lovecraft's Dream Cycle.

## One shared world, played apart

The Dreamlands are an asynchronous shared world, as in Death Stranding: each player plays their own copy at their own pace and never meets another. What others leave flows in: public [[Reverie|Reveries]], planted memories, messages and [[Rumour|rumours]]. Your character is the only [[Lucid]].

## Map and time

The map is continuous, and **time only moves when you move**; when you stand still, the world slowly drifts. The world turns through the [[sleep cycle]] of Light, Deep and Vivid, its version of day and night.

The world is drawn in a painterly 2.5D isometric style that shifts with each place's emotions (see [[Art direction]]).

## Shards and seams

The world's regions are [[Shard|shards]], its most strongly [[Remembered]] and stable parts. Between them run [[Seam|seams]], where [[Forgetting]] has the upper hand. See [[Cosmology]].

## Vertical layers

The dream has **vertical layers**: the waking dream, your dream, and the Dreamer's deep.
