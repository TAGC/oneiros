**Keepsakes** are sentimental heirlooms that act like relics: powerful, but only once you know their story, by being told or by investigating.

## Purely physical

A keepsake is purely physical. It never enters the [[Temenos]] and carries no [[Hold]] or [[Conviction]], unlike the [[Memory|memories]] and [[Belief|beliefs]] you hold. So it can be freely traded (see [[Economy]]).
