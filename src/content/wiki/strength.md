Memories and beliefs share one rule. Each is a form plus amounts of emotional [[material]], in the same units spells use. Its **strength** has two halves: how much (its material) and how rooted ([[Hold]] for a [[memory]], [[Conviction]] for a [[belief]]). Strength is never shown in the interface.

## How rooted

Rootedness sets the tier on the [[rootedness ladder]], and so the kind and permanence of its effect: from a small effect (memory) or none (belief) at [[Loose]], to [[Megaron]], where a memory may surface as a [[mutation]] and a belief's rule becomes permanent.

## How much

Material sets [[mood capacity]]: each emotion's mood capacity is that emotion's material across everything you hold, whatever the tier.

Material times a tier factor (Loose ×0.5, [[Fixture]] ×1, [[Rootedness ladder|Room]] ×2, Megaron ×3) sets its share of maximum [[Coherence]], so a faint but deeply rooted memory still holds you together. The full formula, with [[renown]], is on [[Coherence#Maximum Coherence|Coherence]]; the factors are working values (see [[rootedness ladder]]).

For a belief, only its own material counts towards mood capacity and Coherence, never its [[weight]], so nothing is counted twice.

## Memories and beliefs differ

Memories are snapshots: their composition never changes, and they can only fade or root deeper. Beliefs are fluid: their colour can shift as they soak up feeling (see [[dwelling]]).
