**Other ideas** collects what the vision keeps for later: liked, but not yet worked through.

## Magic

- Magic follows Sanderson's laws: limits matter more than powers (see [[Magic]]).
- Prepared spells: saved casts with a few open slots, alongside live casting (see [[Casting]]).
- Time ratio: each opposed pair governs one aspect of time.
- The dream resists contradiction: heavy magic strains it.
- Feelings might be drawn out of places and people into your mood (see [[Ambient emotion]]).

## World

- Lovecraft: only the shared-dream idea, the world's name and the occasional Easter egg.
- One world, one Dreamer.
- World generation builds a tree of splits: each split divides a [[shard]] along its strongest tension into an outward sibling and an inward one, and [[force]] weakens with every generation (see [[Cosmology]]).
- The world drifts while you are idle; the Dreamer's [[Attention]] speeds it up.
- Regions: a source, a dominant emotion and a local twist.
- Factions: the Wakeners, the Lullaby Choir, the Cartographers and the Dreamt.
- Fragments fade from attention and return simplified.

## Characters

- Origins: Memory-born (the default) and Nightmare-born (the unstable, mutant origin). Origin is now the first step of [[character creation]] (see [[Origin]]).
- Past selves stay in the world as ghosts or legends, and each return makes the Dreamer more aware of you (see [[Echo]]).
- Traits as tagged phrases that stack, synergise, conflict and fuse.
- Unreliable perception for some savants.

## Art and generation

- Composition drives visuals, and the LLM writes creature recipes. Image models only for rare art, generated once and cached (see [[Art direction]] and [[Generation]]).
