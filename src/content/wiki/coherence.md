**Coherence** is health: how well a will keeps its own particular motion instead of breaking apart into separate, simpler motions. In [[Dreamlands|the Dreamlands]] everything has it, by the same rule. You have it, and so does every person, place and thing, including collective wills such as cities.

Damage lowers it. Rest restores it, as do food and potions. Some powerful magic, [[Unmaking]] above all, is paid for in your own Coherence, like blood magic.

## Maximum Coherence

A will's maximum is the [[material]] of every [[memory]] and [[belief]] it holds, each counted by how deeply it is rooted, plus its [[renown]]. The maximum also caps the will's total [[Force|force]].

<p class="formula">Maximum Coherence = Σ (material × tier factor) + renown</p>

| Tier        | Memory       | Belief    | Factor |
| ----------- | ------------ | --------- | -----: |
| Loose       | Impression   | Notion    |   ×0.5 |
| Fixture     | Recollection | Tenet     |     ×1 |
| Room        | Refrain      | Certainty |     ×2 |
| [[Megaron]] | Touchstone   | Dogma     |     ×3 |

A collapsed room counts ×0.5 whatever its tier (see [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]). Only a belief's own material counts, never its [[weight]], so nothing is counted twice.

## In the Temenos

Your [[Temenos]] grows physically with your Coherence. As memories and beliefs root into rooms the house grows, and losing, trading or renouncing them shrinks both.

The Glossary words this differently ("its size is your Coherence"); the conflict is flagged on the [[Temenos]] page.

## Coherence status

Status words show your Coherence at a glance:

<ul class="status status-coherence"><li>Anchored</li><li>Drifting</li><li>Fraying</li><li>Unravelling</li></ul>

## At zero

At zero Coherence you return as an [[Echo]]: weaker, but keeping your [[library]] and what you knew. [[Going under]] is an unlockable alternative, dropping into a deeper layer of the dream for a second chance, for example through a belief such as _death is only a deeper dream_.
