**Oneiros** is a dream journal that becomes a game you can share. It is a systemic RPG in the spirit of Caves of Qud, set in [[the Dreamlands]]: the dream of [[the Dreamer]], a vast, cosmic and unknowable being, shared by everyone who plays.

You write down your real dreams, and they become places in the world that you can explore, grow and share with other players. Around them is a full RPG of emotional magic, shifting [[Rumour|rumours]] and a world that turns through [[Sleep cycle|sleep cycles]].

## Five pillars

Five pillars hold it together.

### Dream world

_A living dream that keeps surfacing new regions._

The Dreamlands are one shared world, played apart: each player plays their own copy at their own pace and never meets another, and what others leave flows in. The world turns through Light, Deep and Vivid, its version of day and night. Its regions are [[Shard|shards]], the parts most strongly [[Remembered]], with [[Seam|seams]] of shifting land between them. See [[The Dreamlands]] and [[Cosmology]].

### Emotion magic

_Eight emotions, each a motion of the will; magic changes how things feel._

Everything in the Dreamlands is a [[will in motion]]: creatures, fire, water, doors, even stone. Raw magic never conjures: it shifts a thing's [[mood]], and the thing acts it out. A fire given despair sinks and dies; a door filled with fear flinches open; a seed given hope lifts and grows. Emotions decide how things move; Memory and Unmaking decide how much they exist. See [[Magic]], [[Emotion]] and [[Casting]].

### Your dreams as Reveries

_The journal is the game's most distinctive feature, though optional._

Record a dream in the [[dream journal]], offer it to the Dreamer, and it becomes a [[Reverie]]: a place grown around your entry as its unchangeable core, which you can keep private, share with particular people or make public. Dreams stay private by default, and the written entry itself is never shared.

### Grows itself

_An LLM fills gaps in the world, always within the engine's rules._

The Dreamer dreams a whole world into existence and its history is simulated; the LLM then adds tagged triggers, effects and places, checked against the rules. It never writes code. See [[Generation]].

### Fair to play free

_Paying buys novelty, never power._

The core game is free. A subscription provides [[apeiron]], the formless stuff of dreams, which pays for new places, people and conversations. [[Attention]] stays dangerous for everyone: apeiron only decides whether what the Dreamer brings is new or familiar.

## You

You play a [[Lucid]]: someone who knows the world is a dream. There is no set story; each world is a new dream of the Dreamer, and its history follows from that dream.

- **Your health** is [[Coherence]]: how well your will keeps its own motion, held up by the [[Memory|memories]] and [[Belief|beliefs]] you hold.
- **Your mind** is a house, the [[Temenos]], where every memory and belief lives and roots deeper (see the [[rootedness ladder]]).
- **Your character** is defined by [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]], and made at [[character creation]] from an [[origin]], a [[temperament]] and a [[backstory]].
- **The Dreamer watches:** [[lucidity]], and beliefs whose rules visibly bend the dream, draw its [[Attention]]. A Lucid who acts like everyone else shows the Dreamer nothing.
