Your **backstory** is your generated starting [[Memory|memories]], precious and finite. It is the third step of [[character creation]], after [[origin]] and [[temperament]].

## At creation

At creation you pick one of three backstory memories to start surfaced (see [[Surfacing]]). Every backstory holds memories of all eight [[Emotion|emotions]], so every floor of your [[Temenos]] starts with at least one room.

## A source of memories

Backstory is one of four sources of memories the vision lists, beside trading, living in the Dreamlands and finding them. The designer has not confirmed that list (see [[Memory]]).
