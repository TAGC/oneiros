**Greek names** belong to the oldest things in the game. Greek is the language of the deep, not the look of the world: it names the oldest, cosmic things (Oneiros, the [[Temenos]], [[apeiron]], [[Oceanus]], Mnemosyne, Lethe) and the units of apeiron.

## The oldest things

| Name      | What it names                                                    |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Oneiros   | The game itself (see [[Oneiros]])                                |
| Temenos   | The house of your mind (see [[Temenos]])                         |
| Apeiron   | The formless stuff of dreams (see [[Apeiron]])                   |
| Oceanus   | The world-river that carries apeiron (see [[Oceanus]])           |
| Mnemosyne | Named only; no role yet                            |
| Lethe     | Named only; no role yet                            |

The units of apeiron are Greek vessels: the [[kyathos]] (a ladle), kotyle (a cup), chous (a pitcher), amphora (a storage jar) and pithos (a great storage jar).

## Lore names

Three Greek names are used only in lore, each for a world that already has a plain name:

| Lore name      | Plain name                                      | What it is                              |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| _Koinos kosmos_ | [[The Dreamlands]]                             | The shared world                        |
| _Idios kosmos_  | [[Inner world]]                                | A will's private world                  |
| _Metaxy_        | [[Meeting ground]]                             | The between where two inner worlds meet |

The same colours of feeling hold in the Dreamlands and in your inner world (see [[Emotion]]).

## Not the look of the world

Greek names do not make the world Greek. Everyday places, people and Reveries follow whatever was dreamt, and there are no Greek gods: [[the Dreamer]] is the only god-figure. How the classical style shapes the Temenos is on [[Art direction]].
