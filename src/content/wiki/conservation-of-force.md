**Conservation of force** is a named law: [[force]] is never created, and is lost only when opposed [[Held and manifest|manifest]] feeling annihilates into [[lucidity]].

When a form that holds force dissolves, its force is freed as [[ambient emotion]]: it keeps its emotion but loses the form that held it.

The law is how the world runs down. [[Force]] owns the rule in full; [[Cosmology]] covers the world's slow loss of force over aeons.
