**Unmaking** is the source of magic that unmakes: it drains feeling, lowers [[Coherence]] and makes things forget. It is paid in your own Coherence, like blood magic, and it is provisional.

Its [[composition]] is the feelings to remove. It is one of three sources, compared on [[Magic#Three sources|Magic]]: emotions decide how things move; Memory and Unmaking decide how much they exist.

## What it frees

Unmaking dissolves form, freeing its feeling into the place as [[ambient emotion]]. The freed force is kept, not lost (see [[Ambient emotion]]).

## Uses

- **Uprooting.** You can roughly [[uprooting|uproot]] something yourself through Unmaking.
- **Scars.** Unmaking clears a [[scar]]'s wound fast, at a cost in your own Coherence, and done carelessly it spreads to the rooms around it.
- **Surfacing.** Unmaking a memory is one way to stop it [[surfacing]].

## Its voice

Unmaking whispers and tempts (see [[Inner voices]]).

## Forgery

The vision says memories can't be forged, "since Unmaking only takes away". The designer has not confirmed this reasoning, and the canon audit notes it no longer holds now that Unmaking frees ambient emotion (see [[Economy]]).
