The **spinning top** is your first [[belief]], _this is a dream_, which is what makes you [[Lucid]]. It stands in the white centre of your [[Temenos]], beside the [[apeiron pool]].

## A fixed Dogma

The spinning top stands at Dogma (a [[Megaron]]) from the start, and nothing can shake it: its [[Conviction]] never changes.

## Effects

Among its effects it steadies your form, raising your [[stability]], so [[lucidity]] and [[Forgetting]] wear on you less.

Holding it is not overt in itself: knowing the world is a dream does not visibly bend the dream, so a Lucid who acts like everyone else shows the Dreamer nothing (see [[Attention]]).
