A **seam** is less [[Remembered]] land between [[Shard|shards]], where the dark of [[Forgetting]] has the upper hand. It shifts far more fluidly than a shard and is more dangerous to cross.

## Crossing

Weakly held things unravel in a seam, while anything strongly held (high maximum [[Coherence]], high [[stability]]) passes with little harm.

## Lucidity along the seam

Neighbouring shards' opposed [[ambient emotion]] meets along a seam and annihilates, so seams shimmer with [[lucidity]], which dissolves the weakly held and frees more. With Forgetting, this is how the world's [[force]] wears down over aeons (see [[Cosmology]]).
