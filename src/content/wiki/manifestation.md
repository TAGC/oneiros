**Manifestation** is anything held in a will's [[inner world]] becoming expressed in the [[Dreamlands]], where it is [[Held and manifest|manifest]].

Held feeling lives inside a will, where opposites coexist. Once it is expressed in the shared world it becomes manifest, and manifest opposites annihilate into [[lucidity]]. [[Held and manifest]] owns that rule.

The plainest case is casting: [[force]] becomes manifest the moment it leaves a [[mood]], so a [[chain]] is only a plan until you cast it.
