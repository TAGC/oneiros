**Oceanus** is the world-river that carries [[apeiron]], the formless stuff of dreams.

Its name is Greek, the language of the deep: Greek names the oldest, cosmic things, such as Oneiros, the [[Temenos]], apeiron and Oceanus (see [[Greek names]]).
