Everything wants to live, and [[memory]] is what holds a being together, so memory is the root of all value, as water is in Caves of Qud. Everyday trade uses **obols**; personal **beads** are goods priced in them.

## Obols

An [[obol]] is a small bead holding a city's founding memory. Calm gives an obol no feeling, so it is useless for conjuring and worth the same to every buyer. How obols are minted, how they shine and how they last is set out on [[Obol]].

## Beads as goods

Personal [[Bead|beads]] are valuable goods priced in obols. A bead's value comes from its shape, how well its feeling suits the buyer's [[mood]] and what [[burning]] it would give. Selling one means you can no longer conjure it. [[Wonder]] memories, new experiences felt deeply, are the most prized.

Memories from others (bought, traded, extracted, or freed when you help someone uproot a traumatic memory and they let you keep it) arrive vivid but Loose, which makes them the readiest fuel for powerful magic. The vision adds that repeated experiences merge into one faint but well-rooted memory, so grinding never pays; this is unconfirmed (see [[Memory]]).

A bead has no [[Hold]], so Hold plays no part in its value. Its condition is the vividness it has left and the quality of its glass (see [[Bead#Condition|condition]]).

## Forgery

Memories can't be forged, since [[Unmaking]] only takes away, but a dealer may pass off a worn bead as fresh; [[Inspect]] shows its true condition.

## Stable materials

Very stable materials such as gold and onyx are prized for building and warding, but they are goods, not money. (On gear made of them, see [[Stability]].)

> [!ISSUE 49]

## What cannot be traded

- [[Keepsake|Keepsakes]] can be freely traded: they are purely physical and carry no Hold or Conviction.
- [[Conviction]] can't be traded. Books in the world carry ideas, not Conviction: reading one gives a [[Musing]].
- [[Apeiron]] is never convertible: it can't be harvested, traded, crafted or turned into memories.
