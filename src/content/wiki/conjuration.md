A **conjuration** is what you make by feeding a [[bead]] emotions from your [[mood]], in the [[Dreamlands]] only. It is a [[manifestation]] made by [[Memory magic]] through [[resonance]].

The memory says what, you say how. A [[memory]] gives only a shape; what you feed it decides how the conjured thing feels and behaves. You pay like any spell.

## What sets a conjuration

- **Shape and senses.** Conjurations take on the memory's sensory details (a fire that smells of baking bread).
- **Detail and duration.** The memory's [[vividness]] sets its detail and how long it lasts.
- **Feeling and behaviour.** What you feed decides how it feels and behaves.
- **Coherence.** What you feed sets its [[Coherence]]; too much risks cracking the bead.

## Manifest

Conjurations are manifest, out in the shared world as stuff (see [[held and manifest]]). When a conjuration ends, its form dissolves but keeps its force, which hangs where it happened as [[ambient emotion]].

## Where you can conjure

Conjuring needs a bead in the Dreamlands, so you prepare the memories you want to use by [[recounting]] them. Selling a bead means you can no longer conjure it. [[Burning]] a memory conjures nothing.

## Kinds of conjuration

- [[Illusion|Illusions]] are flimsy conjurations from faint memories (not yet confirmed; see [[Illusion]]).
- An illusion [[guise]] is a conjuration from a bead holding a memory of the person.

Whether healing and repair need a matching memory is open; see [[Resonance]].
