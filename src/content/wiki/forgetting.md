**Forgetting** is the dark: a constant erosion of memory that surges during the [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]].

[[Memory|Memories]] give off light in their emotion's colour, and wherever this [[memory light]] falls, things stay [[Remembered]] and resist shifting. Whatever slips out of the light, unperceived, may be lost or subtly altered. How memories shine, fade and crack is set out under [[Memory light]].

When a form dissolves through Forgetting, its [[force]] is not lost: it hangs where it happened as [[ambient emotion]].

## In the Temenos

In your [[Temenos]], Forgetting creeps in as dark wherever nothing shines. During a long [[rest]], in the Vivid weak memories are most likely to be lost. A [[scar]]'s Dark malus makes neighbouring rooms lose [[Hold]] faster to Forgetting.

Your [[spinning top]] steadies your form, so [[lucidity]] and Forgetting wear on you less.

A mind of mostly rooted memories is dim, so Forgetting creeps in. This is one of several ways the old die in the [[Dreamlands]], on a timescale longer than a playthrough (see [[Memory light]]).

## In the world

[[Seam|Seams]] are less Remembered land between [[Shard|shards]], where the dark of Forgetting has the upper hand. With the annihilation of opposed [[ambient emotion]] along seams, Forgetting is how the world's force wears down over aeons (see [[Cosmology]]).

## In other minds

When you enter another character's mind, their Forgetting cannot touch you, since you are not their dream (see [[Other minds]]).

## Memory loss

**Memory loss is rare:** severe head injuries, forgetting magic, collapsing.
