**Casting** is how a spell is made. All magic is an emotional [[composition]]. It may also have one or more [[Emotion|emotions]] used as [[Adverb|adverbs]], or none.

## Composition

The composition is any mix of the eight emotions. On the wheel you [[chain]] emotions, and repeats are [[intensity]], up to a chain cap.

Opposites cancel pair by pair, and each cancelled pair releases [[lucidity]]: Hate 4 and Love 2 make Hate 2 and lucidity 2. A chain is only a plan until you cast it, and its opposites cancel at the cast (see [[Held and manifest]]).

## Adverbs

Each adverb is one emotion shaping how the spell acts: Joy _widely_, Courage _steadily_, Hope _far_, Love _wholly_, Grief _slowly_, Fear _fleetingly_, Despair _suddenly_, Hate _deeply_. Each adverb's manner is listed on [[Adverb]].

- Any emotion can be an adverb, including the verb's own, which leans into its natural manner.
- A verb's opposite as adverb cancels into a lucidity dispel.
- An adverb costs a little of its own emotion, always a minor part.
- An adverb scales what the verb needs: _fleetingly_ and _suddenly_ cut the [[material]] required, while _steadily_, _wholly_, _far_ and _widely_ raise it.

## Chained adverbs

A spell is written verbs, then a separator, then adverbs:

<p class="formula">Hate Hate · Far Steadily</p>

A spell with no adverb shows no separator: `Hate Hate`.

The chain cap is shared between the two, so you trade intensity for shaping. Repeating an adverb intensifies it, opposite adverbs cancel into lucidity, and clashing manners play out in order (_suddenly_, then _slowly_).

## Reading a spell

While you build, the spell reads as icons: `Hate Hate · Far Steadily`. The tidied readout collapses each verb run into one intensity word and joins the adverbs:

<p class="formula">Deep Hatred, far and steadily.</p>

Three or more adverbs become a list (_far, widely and steadily_), and a repeated adverb intensifies (_far_, _very far_, _farthest_). The intensity words for each emotion are on [[Intensity]].

## Cost and preview

Icons show intent, not quantities. The engine works out the real cost from the target's size and [[stability]], its [[grain]], the adverb and how far the spell is from your [[mood]], and the preview shows the price before you cast.

Preview works both ways. Build a spell and hover over targets or your inventory to see what it would do to each (spell first), or pick a target and see what each emotion would do to it (target first).

## How things react

Each kind of thing has degrees of freedom, after Bruce Blumberg's model of creature behaviour, and every emotion in the composition pushes on it at once. The strongest gives the primary command; the others act on any free degrees of freedom, or colour how the primary is done. The reactions are set out on [[Kind]], and each degree of freedom is an [[aspect]].

Casting with the [[grain]] lasts; against it, the target's mood drifts back towards its grain. [[Inspect]] shows what each thing feels and which reactions you already know.

## Voices suggest

Your strongest feeling's [[Inner voices|inner voice]] points out what it could do here (Hate notices the wall your enemies lean on). The voices serve the feeling, not you, and following one deepens the [[Quality of the will|quality]] behind its line.
