An **insight** is a realisation you sometimes wake with after [[rest]]. The vision names it beside [[Deduction|deductions]], which you find by placing [[Belief|beliefs]] side by side in the [[library]].

Insights at [[Marvel|marvels]] are one of the sources of Musings, the state a belief is in while you dwell on it (see [[Belief stages#Musing|Musing]]).
