A **scar** is the destroyed form of what was there in your [[Temenos]], at the tier it reached. The house is living, so a scar is a wound: it costs its [[Temenos#Plot|plot]], its [[mood capacity]] and its share of [[Coherence]], and carries **maluses** that mirror what the room gave.

## What scars

Each tier leaves its own ruin:

| Tier                                   | Scar                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| [[Rootedness ladder#Loose\|Loose]]      | None                                    |
| [[Rootedness ladder#Fixture\|Fixture]] | Marks on a wall                         |
| [[Rootedness ladder#Room\|Room]]        | A burnt-out room                        |
| [[Megaron]]                            | A collapsed hall with sagging neighbours |

For a memory Megaron, there is a mark on the body too. Loose items and [[Belief stages|Musings]] leave none.

- **[[Uprooting]]** below Megaron scars only when botched; a Megaron always scars, even when cleanly uprooted.
- **[[Recounting]]** a Fixture too fast risks a scar; too slow risks an interruption instead.
- **A belief** destroyed does what destroying its form would: a bookcase torn out leaves marks on the wall, a burnt room scars the building, and a Dogma's shrine is defaced as its hall collapses (see [[Belief]]).

## Maluses

| Malus       | Effect                                                                                      | From tier |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Dark        | Neighbouring rooms lose [[Hold]] faster to [[Forgetting]]                                   | Fixture   |
| Waking cost | A disturbed [[rest]] restores less                                                          | Room      |
| Ache        | That emotion's [[Inner voices\|inner voice]] speaks of the loss and tugs your mood          | Room      |
| Gap         | A lingering weakness where the effect was: uprooted gills leave you short of breath underwater | Megaron   |

A collapsed room also carries a scar's maluses (see [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]).

## Healing

Scars heal in three stages: **fresh**, **scarred** and **healed**.

- **[[Unmaking]]** clears the wound fast, at a cost in your own Coherence. Done carelessly, it spreads to the rooms around it.
- **Time and help** close it: small scars heal over long rests, and larger ones need someone to tend them.
- **Rebuilding** fills it, by rooting something new of that emotion on the ruin.

Healing can fail. A scar rebuilt with the wrong emotion, or rushed, sets badly and keeps a faint malus, and scar tissue reopens under a trigger like the original wound. Only a healed scar is safe, and it still shows a trace, such as lead clamps across the marble.
