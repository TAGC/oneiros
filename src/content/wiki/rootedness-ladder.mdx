The **rootedness ladder** is the four tiers of how rooted a [[memory]] or [[belief]] is: Loose, Fixture, Room and [[Megaron]]. Every memory and belief lives in your [[Temenos]], and its tier decides its form there and the kind and permanence of its effect.

Rootedness is one half of a thing's [[strength]]: [[Hold]] for a memory, [[Conviction]] for a belief. The other half, its [[material]], sets [[mood capacity]] whatever the tier. Each tier has its own names for memories ([[Memory tiers]]) and beliefs ([[Belief stages]]).

## The four tiers

<SheetTable tab="rootedness" block={0} />

### Loose

A Loose memory is an Impression and a Loose belief is Hearsay: an object on a shelf. Its effect is small for a memory and none for a belief, and destroying it leaves no [[scar]]. Only Loose memories go into [[bead|beads]]: a rooted one must be [[uprooting|uprooted]] first (see [[recounting]]).

### Fixture

A Fixture is built into a room: a hearth, a bookcase. A memory here is a Recollection and a belief a Tenet. Its effect is steadier, and a belief's rule becomes active. Torn out, it leaves marks on the wall.

### Room

A Room is a room of its own, holding loose items and fixtures. A memory here is a Refrain and a belief a Creed. Its effect is strong and lasting, plus a [[waking boon]], and a memory's [[mutation]] is shown in full. Burnt out, it leaves a ruined room.

### Megaron

A [[Megaron]] is a load-bearing great hall taking several rooms' [[Temenos#Plot|plot]]. A memory here is a Touchstone and may [[surfacing|surface]] as a mutation; a belief here is a Dogma, and its rule becomes permanent. Destroyed, it collapses, and always leaves a scar; a memory Megaron also marks the body.

## Rising and falling

Memories and beliefs rise smoothly and fall hard.

- **Memories climb** by being [[reliving|relived]] at high-stakes moments, by [[going under]], in [[Dreamstorm|dreamstorms]] and through [[saturation]].
- **Beliefs climb** through [[dwelling]], seeing them work and the company of believers.

If Hold or Conviction slips below its tier's line, the room cracks; left too long or too far below, it collapses. That rule is set out under [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]].

## Tier factors

Each tier has a factor that sets a thing's share of maximum [[Coherence]]: material times the tier factor, so a faint but deeply rooted memory still holds you together. The working factors are Loose ×0.5, Fixture ×1, Room ×2 and Megaron ×3; the full rule is on [[Coherence#Maximum Coherence|Coherence]].

> [!OPEN]
> The exact tier factors for maximum Coherence are not yet set. The working factors are Loose ×0.5, Fixture ×1, Room ×2, Megaron ×3.
