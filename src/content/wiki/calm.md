**Calm** is the will circling at rest. It is not one of the eight [[emotion|emotions]], which come in opposed pairs. Its colour is soft white.

## In a mood

Every [[mood]] is pictured as a circle whose area is the will's total [[force]]. Each emotion pinches it in its own direction on the wheel; the unpinched rest is Calm, force circling at rest. The [[ring]] beneath every character and thing shows the emotions as lobes in their colours and the rest in Calm's soft white.

After [[Stillness]] passes, your mood resumes as a pure circle of Calm (see [[Wonder]]).

## Calm light

Calm light shelters fading memories with no sparks, unlike opposing coloured light, which sparks into [[lucidity]] where it meets (see [[Memory light]]). An [[obol]] holds a city's founding memory from a deliberately Calm rite: Calm gives it no feeling, so it is faint, useless for conjuring, and its light is a steady white.

## In the Temenos

Calm fills the central room of your [[Temenos]].

The Calm qualities, Humility and Temperance, never urge. After a long quiet, Humility or Temperance may offer one unnamed line of restraint (see [[Inner voices]]).
