**Stability** sets how much an entity's form shifts with its feelings. Gear has stability too.

| Stability | Who                                                    |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Very low  | Beasts and Nightmares                                  |
| Low       | Nightmare-born characters (see [[Origin]])             |
| Medium    | Most people, and Memory-born characters                |
| High      | [[Anchored place\|Anchored places]] and old beings     |

In low-stability origins such as Nightmare-born, [[temperament]] shows in the body itself. The [[spinning top]] raises the stability of your form, so [[lucidity]] and [[Forgetting]] wear on you less.

## Gear

Gear has stability too: cloth reshapes with you, medium gear slips off for a moment, and stable gear such as gold or onyx binds the change at a cost in Fear or [[Coherence]]. How gear's grain meets a change of form is set out under [[Flicker]].

Very stable materials such as gold and onyx are prized for building and warding. The vision calls them goods, not money; the designer has not confirmed that (see [[Economy]]).

## Where stability matters

- **Flickers.** How far a strong mood changes your form is scaled by stability (see [[Flicker]]).
- **Casting.** The engine works out a spell's real cost from the target's size and stability, among other things (see [[Casting]]).
- **Lucidity.** [[Lucidity]] dissolves forms in proportion to how weakly they are held: Coherence, stability and being [[Remembered]].
- **Seams.** Anything strongly held (high maximum Coherence, high stability) crosses a [[seam]] with little harm.
- **Guises.** A guise made by changing your own form is governed by stability, and comes easiest to the Nightmare-born (see [[Guise]]).
