**Surfacing** is the last step of a [[memory]]'s rooting: its roots break through to the exterior, and your body changes in the memory's natural form, with a fixed effect. That change is a [[mutation]]. Memories only: beliefs never surface.

## Warnings

A memory's mutation is hinted as it roots and shown in full at [[Rootedness ladder#Room|Room]]. A memory at [[Megaron]], a Touchstone, may surface.

## The trigger

Surfacing always needs a trigger, usually something traumatic, so a warned player can steer clear. To stop a memory surfacing:

- stop [[reliving]] it;
- [[planting|plant]] it;
- sell it;
- [[unmaking|unmake]] it.

Once surfaced, it is undone by [[uprooting]], which always scars.

## At creation

At [[character creation]] you pick one of three [[backstory]] memories to start surfaced.

> [!CONFLICT]
> The Glossary calls surfacing "a memory's step from Room to Megaron". The vision puts it at Megaron: a Touchstone "may surface as a mutation", and surfacing is "the last step". On the Glossary's reading every Touchstone has surfaced; on the vision's, a Touchstone may not yet have. The vision's version is used until this is settled.
