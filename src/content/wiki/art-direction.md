[[The Dreamlands]] are drawn in a **painterly 2.5D isometric** style that shifts with each place's emotions.

## The Temenos

The [[Temenos]] is a classical marble house: a temple-like exterior of columns and colonnades around the plan of a Hellenistic or Roman house.

- The white centre is its atrium, with the [[apeiron pool]] beneath an opening in the roof that lets in light, never rain.
- The wings open off a colonnaded peristyle.
- Floors are marble and mosaic, lamps burn oil, and amphorae stand by the pool.

[[Quality of the will|Qualities of the will]] restyle this classical base rather than replace it: a Baseness house is a shabby classical house, not a modern one (see [[Quality styles]]).

## Legends

Legends can be drawn like black-figure vase painting.

## Colour

Each emotion has a colour of light, and the same colours hold in the Dreamlands and in your [[inner world]] (see [[Emotion]]). Memories shine in their emotion's colour (see [[Memory light]]).
