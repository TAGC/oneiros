A belief's **weight** is the size of its effect. It is never stored: it is the [[material]] of its [[grounds]] plus its own.

<p class="formula">Weight = material of grounds + own material</p>

Only a belief's own material, soaked up by [[dwelling]], counts towards [[mood capacity]] and [[Coherence]], so nothing is counted twice.

## Light and heavy beliefs

Most beliefs are light: you may be utterly sure of a city's capital, but it weighs almost nothing, while "my mother will always forgive me", resting on vivid Love memories, weighs a great deal without defying anything.

How strong a belief is also decides how much [[Attention]] it draws when its rule bends the dream: flying over a city at noon on a heavy, firm belief draws a lot.
