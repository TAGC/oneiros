An **eidolon** (plural eidola) is the brief form of a [[memory]]'s latent [[mutation]], shown while you are [[Ablaze]] with it, drawback in full. Only vivid enough memories raise one.

It is one of the higher-tier effects a burning memory shows, beside a [[flicker]] and its [[waking boon]]. Whether that list is settled is open; see [[Ablaze#While Ablaze|while Ablaze]].

An eidolon follows the flicker rules for [[stability]] and gear: gear reshapes, slips off or binds the change by its own stability and grain (see [[flicker]]).
