A **mood** is the current feeling of any will (see [[will in motion]]): a volume of every [[emotion]] at once. Every will has a mood, which comes to rest at its [[grain]].

Yours is built by meaningful events and places. [[Raw magic]] and [[Memory magic]] both spend it, and you can only spend what you hold.

## The pinched circle

Every mood is pictured as a circle whose area is the will's total [[force]]. Each emotion pinches it in its own direction on the wheel, only so far (for you, as far as your [[mood capacity]]). The unpinched rest is [[Calm]], the will circling at rest.

Opposites can pinch at once, so they coexist and never net out (see [[Held and manifest]]). Grain is the resting shape the circle relaxes back to, and [[Wonder]] smooths it towards a pure circle.

The [[ring]] beneath every character and thing shows this shape: lobes in each emotion's colour, the rest in Calm's soft white, its size the total force.

## What changes a mood

- A [[stimulus]] or a [[motive]] changes the shape but not the area.
- A [[Raw magic|Raw]] spell that lands carries force: it grows the target's circle and pinches it its way (see [[Stimulus]]).
- Casting spends area from the lobe of the emotion you spend, so the pinch and your total both shrink.
- A will regains force mainly by eating: every living thing exists at the expense of others.

## Limits

Your mood is capped per emotion by its [[mood capacity]]. At an emotion's mood capacity, nothing makes you feel more of it.

Qualities cap how intense a spell can be; mood capacity caps how much of an emotion you have to spend (see [[Intensity]]). [[Burning]] a memory pours its material straight into your mood.
