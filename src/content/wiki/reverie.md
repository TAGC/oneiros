A **Reverie** is a place made from a real dream. You offer an entry in your [[dream journal]] to [[the Dreamer]], who turns it into a Reverie. "Your dreams as Reveries" is one of the game's five pillars (see [[Oneiros]]).

## The core

The original entry is crystallised as the Reverie's unchangeable core. All growth stays true to the core. Everyday places, people and Reveries follow whatever was dreamt, not the Greek of the oldest things (see [[Greek names]]).

## The gateway

Offering an entry makes a new [[gateway]] to the Reverie appear in your [[Temenos]]: one of the free-standing marble gateways ringing the [[apeiron pool]], each fed by a [[euripus]].

## Growth

The Reverie extends as you explore it, around what you do, or while you're away if you let apeiron run to it through its euripus: dry, a trickle or running. The journal and the Reverie are two views of one dream, so adding to the journal grows the world, and what happens in the world is written back into the journal.

## Sharing

A Reverie can stay private, be shared with particular people, or be made public, so it settles into the shared world for others to find. Keep a Reverie closed so only you grow it, or open it for visitors to extend. Public Reveries are among the things that flow into each player's copy of [[the Dreamlands]].

How entries become Reveries, and what stays private, is set out on [[Dream journal]].
