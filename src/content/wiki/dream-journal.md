The **dream journal** is the game's most distinctive feature, though players never have to use it: your real dreams become [[Reverie|Reveries]]. "Your dreams as Reveries" is one of the five pillars of [[Oneiros]].

## From dream to Reverie

1. **Write.** Record a dream in the journal, or talk it through with [[the Dreamer]], who asks questions but never interprets. Writing is free.
2. **Offer.** Spend [[apeiron]] to offer the entry to the Dreamer, who turns it into a Reverie. The original entry is crystallised as its unchangeable core, and a new [[gateway]] to it appears in your [[Temenos]].
3. **Grow.** The Reverie extends as you explore it, around what you do, or while you're away if you let apeiron run to it (see [[Euripus]]). All growth stays true to the core.
4. **Sync.** The journal and the Reverie are two views of one dream. Adding to the journal grows the world, and what happens in the world is written back into the journal.
5. **Share.** A Reverie can stay private, be shared with particular people, or be made public, so it settles into the shared world for others to find. Keep a Reverie closed so only you grow it, or open it for visitors to extend.

## Privacy

Dreams stay private by default. Real people in them become archetypes unless you choose otherwise, and the written entry itself is never shared, only the Reverie made from it. Players also control how much difficult content their dreams can hold.

## The world comes first

As in Dwarf Fortress, the Dreamer dreams a whole world into existence and its history is simulated (see [[Generation]]). The dream journal is optional: record your dreams, then offer them to the Dreamer to weave into the world as Reveries. The generated world is built first, Reveries after.

## Seeds

The game's first dream seeds and recurring motifs come from the designer's own dream journal.
