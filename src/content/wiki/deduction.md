A **deduction** is a finding made by placing [[Belief|beliefs]] side by side in the [[library]] of your [[Temenos]]. It becomes a new belief.

The library holds your colourless beliefs as codices, alongside your [[Thread|threads]]. Sometimes you also wake with an [[insight]].
