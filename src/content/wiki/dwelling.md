**Dwelling** is turning a [[belief]] over in your mind. Like a sponge, a belief draws feeling from your [[mood]], but only the emotions it has an affinity for, set by its [[grounds]] and your [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]], and only up to its [[belief capacity]], set by its grounds. What it soaks up becomes its own [[material]].

Dwelling raises a belief's [[Conviction]], and you steer beliefs by dwelling or doubting.

## When you dwell

You choose what to dwell on only in your [[Temenos]] during [[rest]], a limited number of beliefs at once, and dwelling runs over long spans (days or longer). While you dwell on a belief, it is a Musing: pages on a reading-room desk, bringing a side effect (see [[Belief stages#Musing|Musing]]).

## What it changes

Only a belief's own material counts towards [[mood capacity]] and [[Coherence]], so nothing is counted twice. A belief dwelt on can shift colour, so it may sit on another floor of the house at your next visit.

Burnt material still within your mood capacity stays in your mood after a [[burning|burn]], and dwelling can later soak it into beliefs.

> The sheet says "Losing a ground shrinks it and the excess wrings out". The vision does not say what happens to a belief's own material when it loses a ground.
