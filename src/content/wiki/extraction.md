**Extraction** is taking a [[memory]] or [[belief]] out of someone's mind. It is the dark side of [[Memory magic]], and the opposite of [[inception]], which puts one in. Taking a memory out of a place instead is [[gleaning]].

## How it works

Extraction happens on the [[meeting ground]], where your [[Temenos]] and the target's stand side by side. No [[bead]] is involved: memories move directly between the two houses.

It deals only in Loose memories and beliefs at Notion, since taking anything rooted would mean uprooting it in place (see [[Other minds#What moves]]). Inside their house you take a memory by carrying its lamp, which you can hood to mask its light; a belief at Notion gives no light at all.

## In a heist

Extraction is a step in preparing a heist: you extract memories of the people you will impersonate from secondary targets, then use what you take from the primary target to build [[Guise|guises]] for deeper rooms. A memory taken during the [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]] passes as natural loss.

## What you carry away

Memories from others, extracted ones among them, arrive vivid but Loose, which makes them the readiest fuel for powerful magic (see [[burning]]).
