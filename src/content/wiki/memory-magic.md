**Memory magic** is one of the three sources of [[magic]], beside [[Raw magic]] and [[Unmaking]]. Where Raw magic moves, Memory makes: it conjures what you remember, raises [[Coherence]], heals and repairs. You pay with your [[mood]], and too much power risks cracking the [[bead]]. The three sources are compared on [[Magic]].

## The memory says what, you say how

A [[memory]] gives only a shape. To [[Conjuration|conjure]] it, you feed it emotions from your mood, paid like any spell, and what you feed decides how the conjured thing feels and behaves. The memory's [[vividness]] sets its detail and how long it lasts. Conjurations take on the memory's sensory details (a fire that smells of baking bread).

You conjure from a bead in the Dreamlands by feeding it [[material]] in the memory's own ratio, which [[resonance|resonates]] with the material inside and projects the memory. Nothing in the bead is used up. See [[Resonance]].

## Preparing memories

Conjuring needs a bead in the Dreamlands, so you prepare the memories you want to use, for magic or trade, by [[recounting]] them. While a memory is out of you, its [[mood capacity]] and share of Coherence leave with it (see [[Bead#While a memory is out|while a memory is out]]).

## Other uses of memories

- [[Burning]] a memory pours its material straight into your mood instead, and conjures nothing.
- [[Illusion|Illusions]] come from faint, low-vividness memories (not yet confirmed; see [[Illusion]]).
- A wonder memory is the medium for Stillness magic (see [[Wonder]]).
- [[Extraction]], taking a memory or belief out of someone's mind, is the dark side of Memory magic.
- Raw or Memory magic, cast in the Dreamlands on a sleeping body, can open its mind to reach the [[meeting ground]].
- Memory speaks in recognition among your [[inner voices]].

Whether healing and repair need a matching memory is open; see [[Resonance]].
