In the **cosmology** of Oneiros, the [[Dreamer]] dreamt one unified dream in the beginning. It fractured, and is still fracturing: the resultant motion of the whole world runs towards the Dream's end, far beyond any player's reach.

## Shards of one will

Every being and place is a [[shard]] of that will. Used alone, "shard" means the world's regions: the parts most strongly [[Remembered]], and so the most stable. Between them run [[Seam|seams]], less Remembered land where the dark of [[Forgetting]] has the upper hand.

Only the fabric of the world fractures one way. The history built on it follows patterns like ours, as wills join as well as split, and great kingdoms can rise in any age.

## The world's force

Each will has its own [[force]] (Kraft, Mainländer's term), and the world's force is the sum of theirs. Force is never created and is lost only when opposed manifest motions annihilate (see [[Weakening of force]]).

The world's force diminishes over aeons, too slowly for most inhabitants to notice. Neighbouring shards' opposed [[ambient emotion]] meets along a seam and annihilates, so seams shimmer with [[lucidity]]; with Forgetting, this is how the world's force wears down.

## How the terms fit

| Term | Its part |
| --- | --- |
| [[The Dreamer]] | The being whose fracturing dream is the world |
| [[The Dreamlands]] | That dream, the one shared world |
| [[Shard]] | Any fragment of the Dreamer's will; alone, a stable region |
| [[Seam]] | Less Remembered land between shards, shimmering with lucidity |
| [[Force]] | The amount of a will's motion; the world's is the sum of all wills' |
| [[Weakening of force]] | Why force only ever runs down |
| [[Ambient emotion]] | Freed force, whose opposites annihilate along seams |
| [[Forgetting]] | The dark, with the upper hand in the seams |

[[Other ideas]] lists a further idea, not a decision: world generation might build a tree of splits, each split dividing a shard along its strongest tension into an outward sibling and an inward one, with force weakening every generation.
