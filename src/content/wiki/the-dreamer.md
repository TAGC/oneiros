**The Dreamer** is a vast, cosmic and unknowable being whose dream is the [[Dreamlands]], shared by everyone who plays. There are no Greek gods: the Dreamer is the only god-figure.

There is no set story. Each world is a new dream of the Dreamer, and its history follows from that dream. As in Dwarf Fortress, the Dreamer dreams a whole world into existence and its history is simulated.

## The first dream

In the beginning the Dreamer dreamt one unified dream. It fractured, and is still fracturing, and every being and place is a [[shard]] of that will (see [[Cosmology]]).

## Attention

[[Lucidity]] draws the Dreamer's [[Attention]], and so do [[Belief|beliefs]] whose rules visibly bend the dream. Knowing the world is a dream is not overt in itself, so a [[Lucid]] who acts like everyone else shows the Dreamer nothing. [[Attention]] owns these rules.

## The journal

You can write a dream in the [[dream journal]], or talk it through with the Dreamer, who asks questions but never interprets. Offer the entry with [[apeiron]] and the Dreamer turns it into a [[Reverie]]. With apeiron running to a Reverie along its [[euripus]], the Dreamer grows it while you are away.

Apeiron only decides whether what the Dreamer brings is new or familiar; [[Attention]] stays dangerous for everyone.

## Who knows

The common belief in the world is that the world is real and minds mirror it as a microcosm; a few see it the other way round, but none with the conviction that would make them Lucid. For now, only you know of the Dreamer.

When you enter another character's mind, the target plays the Dreamer's role in their own world (see [[Meeting ground]]).
