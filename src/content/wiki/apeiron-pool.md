The **apeiron pool** is the pool at the white centre of your [[Temenos]] that holds your [[apeiron]]. It lies beneath an opening in the roof that lets in light, never rain, and amphorae stand beside it. Free players see a dry pool, and generative options simply don't appear.

## Around the pool

- Free-standing marble [[Gateway|gateways]] to your [[Reverie|Reveries]] ring the pool. Each is fed by a [[euripus]], a short water channel running out from the pool's rim like a spoke.
- The [[library]] looks down onto it through the light from the centre's upper floor.
- The cellars of the four wings are reached only from below, past the pool.
- The [[spinning top]] stands in the centre too.

## Drawing on it

Fill a bound [[vessel]] from the pool; what you carry is your spending cap in the world. Spilt or stolen apeiron returns to the pool after a sleep cycle (see [[Apeiron]]).

## Refills

The subscription tops the pool up each month, and bought top-ups never expire. Bought top-ups appear as sealed vessels beside the pool, drawn to scale against it; the pool holds a month's subscription. A top-up is at least an amphora, and a large one a great storage jar (a pithos). They stand there until the pool runs low, so you can see your reserve at a glance. Amounts are shown in [[Kyathos|kyathoi]].
