A **shard** is any fragment of the [[Dreamer]]'s original will. The Dreamer's one unified dream fractured, and is still fracturing, so every being and place is a shard of that will (see [[Cosmology]]).

## Regions

Used alone, "shard" means the large regional parts of the world. Shards are the world's regions: the parts most strongly [[Remembered]], and so the most stable. Between them run [[Seam|seams]].

## Where shards meet

Where sibling shards of opposite feeling meet, they annihilate into [[lucidity]]. Neighbouring shards' opposed [[ambient emotion]] meets along a seam and annihilates, so seams shimmer with lucidity.
