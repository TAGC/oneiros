**Wonder** is what happens when you are [[Calm]] near a [[marvel]]: you pass into [[Stillness]]. Your [[ring]] stills, and when Stillness passes your [[mood]] resumes as a pure circle of Calm.

Stillness, the will feeling motionless, is rare and reached through Wonder. [[Stillness]] owns what it is.

## What it does

When Stillness passes:

- your mood resumes as a pure circle of Calm;
- [[Attention]] flares fade, once per marvel per [[sleep cycle|cycle]];
- the place shows its true nature.

Wonder smooths a [[mood]]'s pinched circle towards a pure circle. Like lucidity, it lets you see clearly: [[Ring|rings]] appear when you see through [[lucidity]] or Wonder.

## Wonder memory

Sometimes you keep a **wonder memory**, the rarest kind of [[memory]] and the medium for Stillness magic.

Wonder memories, new experiences felt deeply, are the most prized; the designer has not confirmed this yet (see [[Economy]]).

> Stillness magic, for which a wonder memory is the medium, is not yet described.
