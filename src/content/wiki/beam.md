A **beam** is a [[delivery]] that strikes instantly along a line. It can be one flash or [[Channelling|channelled]].

> [!ISSUE 167]

## Shapes

The [[composition]] sets a beam's effect, and the [[adverb]] sets its shape. With no adverb it is a plain straight beam.

| Adverb       | Shape                                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| _widely_     | Forks                                                        |
| _steadily_   | Holds on its line for several turns                          |
| _far_        | Arcs long over cover, landing a moment later                 |
| _wholly_     | Chains to everything around the target                       |
| _slowly_     | Seeps: weak at first, it keeps burning along its line        |
| _fleetingly_ | Flickers: a quick, cheap flash, with a moment to slip away   |
| _suddenly_   | One flash (see [[Adverb#Suddenly]])                          |
| _deeply_     | Narrows and seeks one target, piercing deepest               |

## Chained adverbs

[[Casting#Chained adverbs|Chained adverbs]] play out in order: `· Far Widely` arcs, then forks; `· Widely Far` forks, then each branch arcs. Opposite adverbs cancel, and repeats intensify. Each adverb applies its own cost change and takes a [[chain]] slot.

## Penetration

How far a beam penetrates depends on its [[intensity]] and on what it passes through.

> [!ISSUE 134]

## Beam struggle

Two opposite beams that meet annihilate at the meeting point, in a constant spray of [[lucidity]]. The stronger stream pushes the meeting point towards the weaker caster.
