**Memory light** is the light every [[memory]] constantly sheds in its emotions' colour. Wherever it falls, things stay [[Remembered]] and resist shifting. Whatever slips out of the light, unperceived, may be lost or subtly altered: that is [[Forgetting]], the dark. Obol light is memory light's steady white kind (see [[obol]]).

The light is the memory's [[material]] running down, in proportion to its composition, so the ratio never changes and [[vividness]] falls. Covering a [[bead]] hides its light but does not slow its fading, and nothing restores vividness. Leaked light becomes trace [[ambient emotion]].

Brightness is the rate of that loss: a fresh, Loose memory blazes and dims fast, and a deeply rooted one glows softly for a long time. In the [[Temenos]], where Loose memories appear as oil lamps, a lamp's brightness shows how fast its memory is fading: a fading memory gutters and a lost one goes out. Held memories light only your Temenos; only beads light the [[Dreamlands]].

## Fading

Inside you, a memory loses a fixed fraction of its light at any moment, smaller the higher its [[Hold]]. [[Rest|Long rests]] raise Hold, weighted towards recent memories, so memories fade ever more slowly as they age (Jost's law). In a bead, a memory fades at a rate set by the glass quality.

## Shelter

Memory light slows the fading of any memory it falls on, by brightness, with diminishing returns and a hard floor, so a cluster lasts longer but never forever. Obols are not inherently durable: they last by clustering.

Where opposing light meets in the Dreamlands it sparks into [[lucidity]], which grazes weakly held things, so a faint, Loose bead may crack. Calm light shelters with no sparks. Inside the Temenos everything is held, so colour plays no part there.

## Cracking

This section is about memories cracking. Rooms in the Temenos also crack, in a different sense: see [[cracking and collapse]].

The weaker a memory, the more likely it is to crack, on a smooth scale:

| Where | Cracks from | Cracks when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside you | Low vividness and low Hold | Mostly during long rests, and from damage to your [[Coherence]] |
| In a bead | Low vividness and poor glass | Over time, and from blows |

In the vision's words, inside you "cracks come mostly during long rests and from damage to your Coherence, which slightly lowers your maximum Coherence". Conjuring with too much power can also crack a bead. A cracked memory spills out as [[ambient emotion]] where it broke, and inside you it stays in the Temenos. No memory simply goes out at a threshold.

## The old

A mind of mostly rooted memories is dim, so Forgetting creeps in. This is one of several ways the old die in the Dreamlands, on a timescale longer than a playthrough.
