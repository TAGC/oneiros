A **mutation** is a lasting change to your form. Only [[memory|memories]] make them. Memories change what you are; [[Belief|beliefs]] change what is true.

## How one comes

A memory's mutation is hinted as it roots and shown in full at [[Rootedness ladder#Room|Room]], where a strong mood can already cause brief [[Flicker|flickers]]: changes of form that are not mutations. The last step, [[surfacing]], always needs a trigger, usually something traumatic, so a warned player can steer clear. Otherwise the memory's roots break through to the exterior and your body changes in the memory's natural form.

While a memory is [[Ablaze]], it can show its [[eidolon]]: the brief form of its mutation, drawback in full.

## What a mutation must be

- Every mutation must add an action, sense or body change, never a bare stat.
- Its drawback is shown as clearly as its benefit.
- Its effect is fixed.

## Beliefs never mutate you

Beliefs never change your form. Their rules become active at Tenet and permanent at Dogma, and you steer them by [[dwelling]] or doubting (see [[belief stages]]).

## Losing one

A surfaced memory is a [[Megaron]], so [[uprooting]] it always leaves a [[scar]], and a memory Megaron also marks the body. Its scar can carry a gap, a lingering weakness where the effect was: uprooted gills leave you short of breath underwater.

## At creation

At [[character creation]] you pick one of three [[backstory]] memories to start surfaced. Nightmare-born characters start with a transformed room (see [[Origin]]).
