The **inspirations** behind [[Oneiros]] come from games, fiction and philosophy. This is what it takes from each.

| Source                               | What we take                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Caves of Qud                         | Systemic depth, layered history, [[Mutation\|mutations]], cooking                                                    |
| Lovecraft's Dream Cycle              | A shared dream world, and the name "the Dreamlands"                                                                  |
| Disco Elysium                        | Skills as [[Inner voices\|inner voices]]; the Thought Cabinet                                                        |
| Brandon Sanderson                    | Magic defined by its limits; savants; storing and [[burning]]                                                        |
| Magicka                              | Chaining elements, with opposites cancelling                                                                         |
| The Wheel of Time                    | Channelling a dangerous source                                                                                       |
| Spiritfarer                          | Caring for others and letting go                                                                                     |
| Philipp Mainländer                   | Everything is [[will in motion]]; feelings as motions; [[Quality of the will\|qualities of the will]]; a world moving towards its end |
| Bruce Blumberg, _Old Tricks, New Dogs_ | Behaviours competing for degrees of freedom, with primary, secondary and meta commands                             |
| Mage: The Ascension                  | Reality pushing back on blatant magic                                                                                |
| Noita, Breath of the Wild            | Systemic world interactions                                                                                          |
| Superhot                             | Time moves only when you move                                                                                        |
| Wildermyth                           | Characters assembled from parts, transforming over time                                                              |
| Brogue                               | Atmospheric lighting and effects                                                                                     |
| Dwarf Fortress                       | World generation with simulated history; layered body parts and clothing                                             |
| The Stormlight Archive               | Storms that shape a world's rhythm                                                                                   |

## Where they show

- Caves of Qud: the game is a systemic RPG in its spirit; memory is the root of all value as water is in Qud (see [[Economy]]); [[Attention]] works like Qud's glimmer; the effects grammar follows Qud's cooking (see [[Crafting]]).
- Blumberg: how things react to a spell, each kind of thing with its degrees of freedom (see [[Casting]] and [[Kind]]).
- Dwarf Fortress: the world is dreamt and its history simulated before any [[Reverie]] (see [[Generation]]).
- Superhot: time only moves when you move (see [[The Dreamlands]]).
