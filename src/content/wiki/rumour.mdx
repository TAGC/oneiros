A **rumour** is something that happened, warped by each teller's [[emotion]]. Every version you hear becomes a [[belief]] at Notion, and enough believers can make a version real.

## How a rumour runs

1. Something happens, often in the [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]].
2. People pass it on, and each teller's emotion warps it: Fear exaggerates the danger, Love adds treasure and allies, Hate assigns blame, Despair predicts doom, Hope promises rescue, and Memory keeps it accurate.
3. Rumours become quests when someone wants something done about them.
4. If enough people believe a version, the dream makes it real, during the Vivid.

## Hearing a rumour

Every version you hear becomes a belief at [[Belief stages|Notion]], and confirming one raises its [[Conviction]]. Rumours are also one source of [[Musing|Musings]]. Your [[library]] keeps the [[Thread|threads]] (quests) hanging off them.

## Where rumours come from

Rumours flow in from other players, along with public [[Reverie|Reveries]], planted memories and messages. [[Planting]] a memory can start rumours of its own. After each [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]], everyone gossips about what changed.
