A **delivery** is where a spell's [[force]] goes. It is one of a spell's three axes (see [[Casting]]).

## Three axes

| Axis          | Says                         |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| [[Composition]] | What the spell does        |
| [[Adverb]]    | How it acts, and its shape   |
| Delivery      | Where its force goes         |

A delivery is not made of [[emotion]]. It is not in the [[chain]], takes no chain slot and has no price of its own.

## The route sets the cost

A delivery's cost comes from its route. A [[bolt]] loses force to the air, an [[aura]] leaks, and a [[touch]] loses nothing. The price preview shows the route's expected losses (see [[Casting#Cost and preview|Casting]]).

## Choosing a delivery

You pick the delivery by input, and the scheme depends on the platform: tap for a one-off spell, hold to [[Channelling|channel]] it. The readout shows the delivery apart from the emotions (see [[Casting#Reading a spell|reading a spell]]).

## The deliveries

| Delivery                        | Goes                                   |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| [[Aura]]                        | Into an area around you                |
| [[Bolt]]                        | As a single shot that flies            |
| [[Beam]]                        | Instantly along a line                 |
| [[Touch]]                       | Into what you are touching             |
| [[#Imbued deliveries\|Imbued]]  | Into a thing, until a trigger releases it |

Any delivery can be one-off or channelled ([[Channelling]]).

"Cast" is not a delivery, since [[casting]] names making any spell.

## Imbued deliveries

Traps, mines, [[potions]] and food are one family: an imbued thing holds a spell in its [[mood]] until a trigger releases it.

| Imbued thing | Released by                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Potion       | Whoever drinks it, who takes the spell in later: a delayed [[touch]] |
| Trap         | The victim's trigger                                   |
| Mine         | The caster's trigger                                   |

The trigger is a variable crafted from the [[Crafting#Effects grammar|effects grammar]] (a basic effect, a trigger and a triggered effect): contact, coming near, opening, a shadow falling, and so on. The [[Crafting#Power budget|power budget]] prices it by how often it fires and how strong it is. Each [[kind]] suggests natural triggers: a threshold when it is opened, light when a shadow crosses it, water when it is disturbed, ground when it is stepped on.

## Inspirations

Magicka's cast types (forward, area, self and weapon) inspired the axis. Skyrim's Creation Kit also uses "Delivery" for the same axis (Self, Touch, Aimed, Target Actor, Target Location) and keeps it apart from Casting Type. The imbued family borrows from Magicka's mines (Shield with Life or Arcane) and from Path of Exile, whose traps go off by proximity or duration and whose mines go off by hand.
