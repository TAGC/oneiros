**Capacity** is the most something can hold. It is always qualified:

- **[[Mood capacity]]:** how far your [[mood]] can pinch towards one emotion.
- **[[Belief capacity]]:** the most [[material]] a [[belief]] can soak up through [[dwelling]].
- **[[Vessel capacity]]:** how many [[Kyathos|kyathoi]] a [[vessel]] holds.

Mood capacity is separate from how intense a spell can be: qualities cap how intense a spell can be; mood capacity caps how much of an emotion you have to spend.
