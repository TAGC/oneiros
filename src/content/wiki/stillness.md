**Stillness** is the will feeling motionless, after Mainländer's aesthetic contemplation: its [[force]] is intact and still moving, but its motion is no longer felt from inside. It is a state, not a loss.

It is rare, reached through [[Wonder]]: be Calm near a [[marvel]] and you pass into Stillness. Your [[ring]] stills, and when Stillness passes your [[mood]] resumes as a pure circle of [[Calm]]. What else happens then is set out on [[Wonder]].

## Stillness magic

Sometimes you keep a wonder memory, the rarest kind, which is the medium for Stillness magic (see [[Wonder]]).

## Voices

Holiness speaks once in Stillness (see [[Inner voices]]).
