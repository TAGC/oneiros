**Inspect** is examining a scene to see what each thing feels, its [[kind]], [[grain]] and [[undertone]], and which reactions you already know. Unknown reactions show as ?.

[[Ring|Rings]] appear when you inspect, open the wheel, or see clearly through [[lucidity]] or [[Wonder]].

## Studying a memory

Inspect also studies a [[memory]] where it sits without taking it, including in someone else's [[inner world]] (see [[Other minds]]). What it reveals deepens with the time you spend, what you already know (your beliefs and memories about what you inspect) and the memory's [[vividness]].

Some beliefs and memories also make you better at inspecting certain kinds of thing in general.

## Beads and features

The vision says a dealer may pass off a worn [[bead]] as fresh, and Inspect shows its true condition. The designer has not confirmed this (see [[Economy]]).

In the [[Temenos]], inspecting a feature describes how it behaves (see [[Quality styles]]).
