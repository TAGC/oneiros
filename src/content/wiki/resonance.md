**Resonance** is how a [[bead]] projects its [[memory]] in [[conjuration]]. You conjure from a bead in the [[Dreamlands]] by feeding it [[material]] in the memory's own ratio, which resonates with the material inside and projects the memory.

Nothing in the bead is used up or restored: the bead holds the form. Use leaves no mark, so a bead conjured from many times at safe power is the same as an unused bead of the same age.

## The ratio

Only the memory's own ratio works, exactly. A memory of 70 Joy and 30 Grief resonates only with Joy and Grief fed at 70 to 30; a near ratio such as 60 to 40 projects nothing, and no twisted version comes out. The amount is free: any amount in that ratio works.

## What you feed

The more material you feed, the more coherent the conjuration: what you feed sets its [[Coherence]]. Too much stresses the bead and risks cracking it (see [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]]). You pay for it from your [[mood]], like any spell (see [[Memory magic]]).

Healing and repair need a match.

> [!OPEN]
> That healing and repair need a match was accepted only in a bulk agreement. The designer has not confirmed it, and the vision does not say what must match.

> [!OPEN]
> Whether heavy use should wear a bead down is not settled; the designer is unsure. The old reference sheet had one form of it: feeding against a memory's feeling "twists the result and wears faster".
