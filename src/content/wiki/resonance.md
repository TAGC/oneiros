**Resonance** is how a [[bead]] projects its [[memory]] in [[conjuration]]. You conjure from a bead in the [[Dreamlands]] by feeding it [[material]] in the memory's own ratio, which resonates with the material inside and projects the memory.

Nothing in the bead is used up or restored: the bead holds the form. Only the memory's own ratio works.

## What you feed

What you feed sets the conjuration's [[Coherence]]; too much stresses the bead and risks cracking it (see [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]]). You pay for it from your [[mood]], like any spell (see [[Memory magic]]).

Healing and repair need a match.

> [!OPEN]
> That healing and repair need a match was accepted only in a bulk agreement. The designer has not confirmed it, and the vision does not say what must match.
