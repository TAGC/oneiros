A **kind** is a category of thing with its own reactions: fire, water, air and weather, growth, solid, threshold, light and creature. Each [[emotion]] has a reaction on each kind, and individual things can have their own.

## How things react

How things react follows Bruce Blumberg's model of creature behaviour. Each kind of thing has degrees of freedom, and every emotion in the [[composition]] pushes on it at once. The strongest gives the primary command; the others act on any free degrees of freedom, or colour how the primary is done. Each degree of freedom is an [[aspect]].

Joy makes a plant bloom, Hate grows it thorns, Fear makes it tremble and grow stunted.

[[Inspect]] shows a thing's kind, and which of its reactions you already know; unknown ones show as ?.
