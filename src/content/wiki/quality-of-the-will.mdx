Your character is defined by **qualities of the will**, after Philipp Mainländer's opposed pairs: Envy and Good will, Cruelty and Mercy, Cowardice and Audacity, Fidelity and Falseness, and so on. Qualities decide which [[Emotion|emotions]] come most naturally to you: where your [[mood]] rests, and which way it drifts. Things have qualities too (see [[#Qualities of things]]).

Above the qualities sits your [[temperament]], which shapes which qualities come easily but never forbids any, since everyone carries the seed of every quality.

## Depth

Each quality has a depth that deepens with use or fades, leaving remnants. Following an [[Inner voices|inner voice]] deepens the quality behind its line.

The depth of the qualities that feed an emotion sets:

- **its floor's plot** in your [[Temenos]] (see [[Temenos#Plot|plot]]);
- **how high an intensity** you can cast it at. Qualities cap how intense a spell can be; [[mood capacity]] caps how much of an emotion you have to spend (see [[Intensity]]).

## What qualities shape

- **Your house.** Qualities of the will style the whole Temenos (see [[Quality styles]]).
- **Your voices.** What an inner voice wants comes from the quality feeding it (see [[Inner voices]]).
- **Your reactions.** Qualities decide which reaction a [[motive]] gives rise to.
- **Your beliefs.** A belief's affinity, the emotions it can soak up by [[dwelling]], is set by its grounds and your qualities.

## Resistance

A will's qualities make it resist damage of the emotions they feed, and weak to each one's opposite only. Several qualities add up: a will with Envy and Cruelty, which both feed Hate, resists Hate strongly and is clearly weak to Love. This is tentative.

Harm from being pushed past a [[mood capacity]] is a different thing. It depends on how much room a will has, not on its qualities (see [[Outburst#Harm|outburst]]).

## Qualities of things

Every kind of thing has qualities, drawn as a fitting subset from the one list below. Individuals can vary from their [[kind]]. Qualities tilt a thing's mood, and set its [[grain]]; they never limit which emotions it can hold.

These subsets are suggestions, not yet settled:

| Kind            | Qualities                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Fire            | Greed, Audacity                            |
| Water           | Malleability, Meekness                     |
| Air and weather | Malleability, Falseness                    |
| Growth          | Defiance, Sensuality                       |
| Solid           | Inflexibility, Obduracy                    |
| Threshold       | Obduracy when shut, Candour when open      |
| Light           | Candour, Generosity                        |
| Creature        | By species                                 |

In Mainländer's philosophy every thing is an individual will, but the named qualities come from his study of the human will (a reading not yet checked, about p. 54 of _The Philosophy of Redemption_). Giving them to things is the game's choice.

## The qualities

The sheet lists fifteen pairs, the emotion each quality feeds and how it deepens in play. In the sheet most feed one of the eight emotions, while Humility and Temperance feed [[Calm]] and Holiness feeds [[Stillness]]; the vision calls these three the Calm qualities, which never urge (see [[Inner voices]]).
