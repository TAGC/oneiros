**Ambient emotion** is feeling no will holds: [[force]] freed when any form dissolves, keeping its emotion and hanging where it happened (a hall thick with Despair). Inside the [[Temenos]] it stays in the Temenos.

It is distinct from a place's [[mood]], which the place's own will holds.

## Where it comes from

Any form that dissolves keeps its force, which hangs where it happened as ambient emotion. Forms dissolve through:

- [[Forgetting]];
- [[Unmaking]], which dissolves form, freeing its feeling into the place;
- fading: a memory's leaked [[Memory light|light]] becomes trace ambient emotion;
- [[uprooting]];
- a [[conjuration]] ending;
- [[Held and manifest#Shaped feeling|shaped feeling]] ending, or an [[aura]] leaking;
- [[lucidity]], whose cascades are described below.

An [[outburst]] also becomes ambient emotion: once it has left the body it hangs around the will it left, and lands in no will.

A memory can also spill out as ambient emotion. A cracked memory spills where it broke (see [[Memory light]]), and an interrupted transfer floods its material out as ambient emotion (see [[Recounting]]).

## Manifest feeling

Ambient emotion is [[Held and manifest|manifest]], so opposites cancel in it into lucidity. What lucidity dissolves releases its force as more ambient emotion, which can cancel into more lucidity, so cascades are possible. A Hope spell cast through Despair-thick air loses some of itself before it lands.

Along a [[seam]], neighbouring [[Shard|shards]]' opposed ambient emotion meets and annihilates, so seams shimmer with lucidity.
