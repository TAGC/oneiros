**Vividness** is a [[memory]]'s total [[material]]: its detail and feeling. It comes from novelty and feeling together: an experience without feeling fades, and so does a feeling you have had many times.

Vividness is the "how much" half of a memory's [[strength]]; [[Hold]] is the "how rooted" half.

## Falling

Every memory constantly sheds [[memory light]] in its emotions' colour. The light is its material running down, in proportion to its composition, so the ratio never changes and vividness falls. Nothing restores vividness. A fresh, Loose memory blazes and dims fast; a deeply rooted one glows softly for a long time. See [[Memory light#Fading|fading]].

## What vividness does

- **Conjuring.** Vividness sets a [[conjuration]]'s detail and how long it lasts. [[Illusion|Illusions]] come from faint, low-vividness memories (not yet confirmed; see [[Illusion]]).
- **Coherence.** With its tier, vividness sets the memory's share of your maximum [[Coherence]].
- **Cracking.** The lower its vividness, the more likely a memory is to crack, inside you or in a bead (see [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]]).
- **Transfer.** Memory flows at a fixed rate, so vivid memories take longest to [[Recounting|recount]] or [[Reliving|relive]].
- **Burning.** Only vivid enough memories raise an [[eidolon]] while you are [[Ablaze]].
- **Inspecting.** What [[Inspect]] reveals about a memory deepens with its vividness.

Your own vivid memories are usually deeply rooted, so they are hard to [[Burning|burn]]; vivid memories from others arrive Loose.

The vision also says repeated experiences merge into one faint but well-rooted memory. This is not confirmed and clashes with the rule above that a feeling had many times fades; see [[Memory#Merging repeated experiences|merging]].
