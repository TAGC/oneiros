A **Megaron** (plural **Megara**) is the top tier of the [[rootedness ladder]]: a load-bearing great hall in your [[Temenos]], taking several rooms' worth of [[Temenos#Plot|plot]]. A [[memory]] at this tier is a Touchstone (see [[memory tiers]]); a [[belief]] is a Dogma (see [[belief stages]]). You can have several.

## Effect

- **A memory Megaron** may [[surfacing|surface]] as a [[mutation]]: its roots break through to the exterior, and your body changes.
- **A belief Megaron** makes the belief's rule permanent. A Dogma about yourself permanently changes the rules about you, though never your form. It takes the form of a great hall around a sealed shrine, holding a codex bound in bronze boards.

Its material counts ×3 towards maximum [[Coherence]], the highest working factor (see [[Coherence#Maximum Coherence|maximum Coherence]]).

Your first belief, the [[spinning top]] (_this is a dream_), stands at Dogma from the start.

## Losing one

A Megaron always scars. Destroyed, it collapses: a collapsed hall with sagging neighbours, and for a memory Megaron a mark on the body too (see [[Scar]]). [[Uprooting]] a Megaron always leaves a scar, even when it is done cleanly. Like any room, it can also crack and collapse if its Hold or Conviction slips below its tier's line (see [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]).

Rooms and Megara cannot be burnt; the designer has not confirmed this for Megara (see [[Burning]]).
