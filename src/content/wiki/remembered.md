**Remembered** is the state of things within [[memory light]]: stable and resistant to shifting. Wherever memory light falls, things stay Remembered. [[Obol]] light is its steady white kind: cities hang obols as street lamps through the [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]], so a city's coin literally keeps it from shifting.

Whatever slips out of the light, unperceived, may be lost or subtly altered by [[Forgetting]].

## Holding against lucidity

Being Remembered is one of the ways a form is strongly held, with [[Coherence]] and [[stability]]. [[Lucidity]] dissolves forms in proportion to how weakly they are held, so Remembered things resist it.

## Across the world

[[Shard|Shards]], the world's regions, are its most strongly Remembered parts, and so the most stable. The [[Seam|seams]] between them are less Remembered land, where the dark of Forgetting has the upper hand.
