Some quests take you into another character's [[Temenos]], with their permission, to help with a troubling [[memory]]: by revealing what really happened, calling up who they lost, restoring something good, helping them [[Uprooting|uproot]] it, or the dangerous shortcut of erasing it.

The same way in serves intrusion. [[Extraction]] and [[inception]] happen on the [[meeting ground]] (in lore, _Metaxy_): a small space where two [[Inner world|inner worlds]] meet, holding your Temenos and the target's side by side. No [[bead]] is involved: memories move directly between the two houses.

## Getting in

You reach the meeting ground during the target's long rest, either with their permission or by opening their mind with [[Raw magic|Raw]] or [[Memory magic]]. A visit costs you a long rest's benefits. The full rule is on [[Meeting ground#Getting in]].

Inside, the target plays [[the Dreamer]]'s role in their own world, and acts that don't fit draw their [[Attention]]. At [[Fixed upon]] the meeting ground collapses and throws you out (see [[Meeting ground#Their Attention]]).

## Light and dark

Lit areas are where the target's memories shine. There you are seen, so you must play a part; suspicious actions belong in the dark. You arrive dark, carrying no light of your own, and the target's [[Forgetting]] cannot touch you, since you are not their dream.

Memories are lamps: you take or leave one by carrying it, as you would a real lamp, and you can hood it to mask its light. Beliefs give no light, so a belief at Notion, a loose leaf on a shelf, can be taken or left entirely in the dark.

## Guises

In the light you wear a [[guise]]: the likeness of someone the target remembers, with your mood matched to how they feel about that person. You switch guise in the dark.

## Preparation

A heist builds in stages:

1. Learn about the people you will impersonate by talking to people, reading and breaking in to go through diaries.
2. Extract memories of them from secondary targets.
3. Enter the primary target, starting with loosely guarded memories and using what you take to build guises for deeper, more tightly guarded rooms.

[[Inspect]] lets you study a memory where it sits without taking it; what it reveals deepens with the time you spend, what you already know and the memory's [[vividness]]. Going slowly costs time, and every visit risks being caught.

## What moves

Extraction and inception deal only in Loose memories and beliefs at Notion; taking anything rooted would mean uprooting it in place. An incepted memory arrives Loose and an incepted belief at Notion, and they root or fade through the target's long rests like anything else they hold. (See the [[rootedness ladder]] for the tiers.)

## When you go in

When you go in matters. A house's phases mirror the world's [[sleep cycle]]: [[Sleep cycle#Light|Light]], [[Deep]] and [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]].

| Phase  | In the house                                                                                                 | Getting in           | Once inside                                                                                                                 |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Light  | Calm; the sleeper is near waking                                                                             | Easiest              | Hardest: Attention climbs fastest, and there is least darkness                                                              |
| Deep   | Still and heavy; recent memories consolidate; the house's depths come closer (tentative)                      | Hardest and riskiest | Easiest: Attention climbs slowest                                                                                           |
| Vivid  | Weak memories are discarded, the house reshuffles, and the sleeper's dreams manifest as hostile creatures     | Fairly easy          | Dangerous and hard to navigate, but the most darkness, and you stand out least; a memory taken now passes as natural loss  |

See the open question on [[Rest#issue-98|Rest]].

## Practitioners

Mind intrusion is known but rare. Practitioners enter others' houses with permission as a trade, to uproot memories and tend [[Scar|scars]], and some turn intruder.

The common belief is that the world is real and minds mirror it as a microcosm; a few see it the other way round, but none with the conviction that would make them [[Lucid]]. For now, only you know of the Dreamer.
