**Hypnopompia** is the last explorable phase of a [[Rest#Long rest|long rest]], as your character wakes. You explore your [[Temenos]] to see what changed overnight, before waking fully.

It follows [[hypnagogia]] and the fast-forward through sleep, in which the house changes: recent memories consolidate in the [[Deep]], and in the [[Sleep cycle|Vivid]] weak memories are most likely to be lost and the house reshuffles (see [[Rest]]).

Sometimes you wake with an [[insight]].
