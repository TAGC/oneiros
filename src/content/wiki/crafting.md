**Crafting** is wide, not tall: bottling, imprinting, materials, anchors, artifice. Two systems underlie it, and much else besides: the effects grammar and the power budget, which also keeps [[generation|generated]] content balanced.

## Effects grammar

The **effects grammar** is a basic effect, a trigger and a triggered effect, as in Qud's cooking. It is used for food, [[potions]], [[Memory|memories]], items and artefacts.

## Power budget

Under the **power budget**, every effect costs points by how often it fires and how strong it is, so generated content stays balanced.

## Gear

Gear can amplify, protect, widen or store, using the shared effects grammar. It is worn in body-part slots with a core layer and a persona layer (see [[Core and persona]]), and has [[stability]] of its own.

> [!OPEN]
> The designer has not confirmed that gear can amplify, protect, widen or store.

## Ingredients

The optional garden in your [[Temenos]] grows ingredients between rests. How food compares with potions is on [[Potions#Food and potions]].

> The vision names the crafts (bottling, imprinting, materials, anchors, artifice) but does not yet describe them.
