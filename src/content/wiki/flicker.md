A **flicker** is a brief change of form under a strong [[mood]] while a memory sits at [[Rootedness ladder#Room|Room]], scaled by [[stability]]. Flickers are not [[Mutation|mutations]].

A memory's mutation is hinted as it roots and shown in full at Room, where a strong mood can already cause flickers. Only the last step, [[surfacing]], makes the change lasting.

## Gear

Gear reshapes, slips off or binds a flicker, by its own stability (see [[Stability#Gear]]) and its [[grain]]. Gear whose grain matches a flicker welcomes it; opposed grain resists and damps it, and nothing cancels.

## While Ablaze

While you are [[Ablaze]], a burning memory shows effects it would have at a higher tier, and the vision lists a flicker among them. The designer has agreed only the principle (see [[Ablaze]]).
