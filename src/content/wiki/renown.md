**Renown** is how well the world remembers someone or something: the [[material]] of the [[Memory|memories]] and [[Belief|beliefs]] others hold about it, times a multiplier still to be set.

It adds to the maximum [[Coherence]] of anyone or anything: you, a person, a city, a place.

> [!OPEN]
> The multiplier on renown is not yet set.
