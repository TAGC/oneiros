A **touch** is a [[delivery]] by contact. Nothing is lost on the way, and it cannot be met in flight. It reaches one target only, and you must be within reach.

## Opposites

Like any delivery, a touch is [[Held and manifest|manifest]] for an instant; it is no exception to held and manifest. So:

- opposites in one touch cancel at the cast;
- two opposite touches that land at the same instant annihilate on the target;
- to put opposites into one will, touch in turn, or touch with both hands, one emotion in each, as [[Held and manifest#Casting opposites at one target|twin casting]] does.

Touching manifest feeling with its opposite annihilates it at your palm into [[lucidity]], which draws [[Attention]] to where you stand.

## Touch and inception

Touch moves spells, which are manifest [[force]]. [[Inception]] moves held memories and beliefs. Touch must not duplicate inception.

## Spreading and traces

Outward emotions spill a little from the target to whatever it touches; inward ones stay in the one target. Contact is mutual: the toucher takes a weak trace of the target's strongest emotion.

## Imbuing

Touching a thing loads its [[mood]] with the spell, as held feeling. It lasts if it goes with the thing's [[grain]], and drifts back against it. Traps, mines and potions are imbued things (see [[Delivery#Imbued deliveries|imbued deliveries]]).

## Channelled touch

A [[Channelling|channelled]] touch flows while contact lasts. The adverbs _far_ and _widely_ shape the reaction, not the reach.
