A **thread** is a quest hanging off a [[rumour]] or other [[belief]], kept in the [[library]] of your [[Temenos]]. It is not a belief itself.

Rumours become quests when someone wants something done about them. Every version of a rumour you hear becomes a belief at Hearsay, and your library keeps the threads hanging off them, tied to the beliefs they hang off.
