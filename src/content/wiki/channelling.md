**Channelling** is keeping a spell flowing for as long as you keep it up. It is a timing, not a [[delivery]]: any delivery can be one-off or channelled.

## By delivery

| Delivery   | Channelled                                                      |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Aura]]   | Feeds force in while it leaks out, and recovers from damage (see [[Aura#Leak]]) |
| [[Beam]]   | A flow along the line instead of one flash                      |
| [[Touch]]  | Flows while contact lasts                                       |

A spell cast _suddenly_ goes out all at once, so it cannot be channelled (see [[Adverb#Suddenly]]).

## The word

In games, channelling almost always means holding a spell to sustain it, as in Path of Exile, World of Warcraft and Dota; D&D's concentration is close.
