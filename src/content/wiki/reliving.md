**Reliving** draws a [[memory]] from a [[bead]] back into your mind. Like [[recounting]], its opposite, it happens anywhere in the [[Dreamlands]], never inside the [[Temenos]].

Reliving leaves the bead [[dun]] and pinches your [[mood]] towards the memory's emotions. The memory comes back Loose.

Reliving follows the same transfer rules as recounting: a fixed rate of flow, concentration, and the whole memory lost if the transfer is interrupted. See [[Recounting#Transfers|transfers]].

## Relived at high-stakes moments

The vision also uses "relived" in a wider sense. Memories climb the [[rootedness ladder]] by being relived at high-stakes moments, among other ways (see [[Hold]]). A memory heading towards [[surfacing]] can be stopped by no longer reliving it, among other ways.

> [!OPEN]
> The vision does not say whether being "relived at high-stakes moments", or reliving that leads towards surfacing, is the same act as drawing a memory from a bead.
