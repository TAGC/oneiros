An **aspect** is one way a thing can vary: a door's lock, a fire's height, a room's light. Each degree of freedom a [[kind]] of thing has is an aspect, after Bruce Blumberg's degrees of freedom.

## Drivers

Each aspect responds to a driver. A thing's aspects respond to its [[mood]]; the [[Temenos]]'s respond to your [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]] (see [[Quality styles]]).

On each aspect the strongest claimant sets the main form; weaker ones take its sub-aspects or colour the manner, as in spell resolution (see [[Casting#How things react|how things react]]).

## How an aspect responds

An aspect responds in one of these ways:

- **Continuously.**
- **Latched.** A latch can release at a different line from the one that trips it, and some latches are one-way.
- **Staggered**, stepping at thresholds.
- **From several drivers at once.**

Where the lines sit is tuning.

## Opposites

Opposites in a mood never net out, though an aspect can read two against each other: Courage against Fear for holding fast.
