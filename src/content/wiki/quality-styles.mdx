**Qualities of the will style the whole house.** Each [[Quality of the will|quality]] has a profile of how it shapes your [[Temenos]]: doors, windows, ceilings, furniture, mirrors, sound and more, plus unique features that unlock at its own depths or after deeds (Envy's gallery of empty frames, Imperiousness's throne).

For now all styling is cosmetic: it shows who you have become but changes nothing you can do. Mechanical effects (sidegrades, each gain paired with its own cost) will be revisited once more mechanics are settled.

## How styling works

- **Aspects.** The Temenos's [[aspect|aspects]] respond to your qualities of the will, as a thing's aspects respond to its mood. On each aspect the strongest quality sets the main form and weaker ones claim details or the manner, as in spell resolution (see [[Casting]]). Clashes show as compromise or tension.
- **Count, look, behaviour.** A feature's count comes from the qualities, its look from the room and its behaviour from the quality.
- **Spread.** Influence spreads continuously from a quality's home floor as it deepens.
- **Fading.** A deep quality that fades leaves remnants.
- **Inspecting.** [[Inspect|Inspecting]] a feature describes how it behaves.
- **Symbols.** Classical symbols appear as traces, motifs and behaviour rather than emblems.

## A classical base

The Temenos is a classical marble house, and qualities restyle this classical base rather than replace it: a Baseness house is a shabby classical house, not a modern one (see [[Art direction]]).

## Reading the tables

The two tables below are the sheet's detail. **Quality styles** gives each quality's symbols, its house if it were the only quality, its signature detail, what appears as it deepens and what is left if it fades. **Feature claims** gives, for each aspect of the house, how strongly each quality claims it (Strong, Medium or Weak) and how.

Codes in the depth columns:

- **(C)**: continuous, spreading as the quality deepens. "Pervasive" marks one that spreads beyond its wing.
- **(U: …)**: unlocked by the deed or threshold named, once or once per deed.

> [!OPEN]
> The sheet does not define these codes. The reading above is inferred from the vision ("Influence spreads continuously … unique features that unlock at its own depths or after deeds"), and the designer has not confirmed it.

> [!OPEN]
> The depth stages (Early, Growing, Deep, and "Marked" in many cells) appear in neither the vision nor the Glossary, which speak only of a quality's "own depths". Whether Marked is the same as Growing is unclear, and "Deep" clashes with the [[Deep]] sleep phase. The stage names need a Glossary decision.

## Corrections and open questions

> [!CONFLICT]
> Both tabs called the quality "Mercy". The vision's pair is "cruelty and mercifulness", and the Glossary agrees, so the tables now say Mercifulness. The vision's version is used until this is settled (see [[Quality of the will]]).

> [!CONFLICT]
> Humility's Deep cell said a settled hush "fills the Calm wing". The vision has four wings, one per emotion pair, and "Calm fills the central room", so the table now says "fills the centre". The vision's version is used until this is settled.

> [!OPEN]
> Several cells describe wooden floors (Baseness: "worn, uneven boards"; Audacity: "Floorboards ring like drumskins"; Despondency's remnants: "warped floorboards"). The vision says the house's "floors are marble and mosaic", and qualities restyle the classical base rather than replace it. This may be a conflict.

> [!OPEN]
> Inflexibility's "Candle flames stand perfectly still" and Temperance's "One candle per room" sit uneasily with the vision's "lamps burn oil", and risk confusion with memories, which appear as oil lamps.

> [!OPEN]
> Some deep features touch navigation or plot, for example Cowardice's "every room grows a second way out", Obduracy's "no new doorway can be cut" and Defiance's "run, and corridors lengthen". The vision says all styling is cosmetic for now, so these are read as cosmetic. Whether any of them should change what you can do waits for the mechanical effects.

> [!OPEN]
> Humility's mirrors "are taken down one by one" at depth, and Holiness has "No mirrors", while the sheet's Temenos tab lists a Mirror as a fixed room (see [[Temenos]]). The sheet is in tension with itself here.
