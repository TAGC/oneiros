**Resting is tending.** Rest restores [[Coherence]], mends wounds and turns recent experiences into [[memory|memories]]. You rest by visiting your [[Temenos]]. The world does not rest when you do.

## Long rest and short rest

### Long rest

**Long rests** are limited, as in D&D. A long rest is sleep through a full [[sleep cycle]]:

1. You explore your Temenos in [[hypnagogia]].
2. You watch it change in a fast-forward through sleep.
3. You see what changed in [[hypnopompia]].
4. Then you wake.

Only long rests change the Temenos.

### Short rest

**Short rests**, visiting your Temenos whenever you are out of immediate danger, are unlimited but restore less. During short rests nothing roots or reorganises.

## Tending

- **Automatic tending** works through priorities and ends with a [[waking summary]]: what was restored, what you remembered, what still needs time, and what happened while you slept.
- **Manual tending** offers choice, discovery and decoration, never more power.

Each rest grants a [[waking boon]] from a tended room.

## Phases of a long rest

The Temenos's phases mirror the world's. In the [[Deep]], recent memories consolidate, their [[Hold]] rising (weighted towards the most recent). In the [[Sleep cycle|Vivid]], weak memories are most likely to be lost and the house reshuffles.

| Phase                       | In the house                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Sleep cycle#Light\|Light]] | Calm; the sleeper is near waking                                                                     |
| [[Deep]]                    | Still and heavy; recent memories consolidate; the house's depths come closer (tentative)             |
| [[Sleep cycle\|Vivid]]       | Weak memories are discarded, the house reshuffles, and the sleeper's dreams manifest as hostile creatures |

The full table, with what each phase means for getting into someone else's house, is on [[Other minds]].

> [!OPEN]
> That the house's depths come closer in the Deep is marked "tentative" in the vision.

## What rest touches

- **Memories.** Memories you live in the Dreamlands are consolidated during long rests, or they fade. Long rests raise Hold, weighted towards recent memories (see [[Memory light]]). Inside you, memories crack mostly during long rests.
- **Beliefs.** You choose what to [[dwelling|dwell]] on only in your Temenos during rest.
- **Scars.** Small [[scar|scars]] heal over long rests. The Waking cost malus means a disturbed rest restores less.
- **Insights.** Sometimes you wake with an [[insight]].
- **The garden.** The optional garden grows ingredients between rests.
- **Potions.** Potions are brewed at rest, with care for a clean result (see [[Potions]]).
- **Other minds.** Your house is not tended while you infiltrate someone else's, so a visit costs you a long rest's benefits (see [[Other minds]]).
