> **Superseded, not canon.** Everything on this page belongs to an earlier design of the magic system. None of it is a decision, and where it disagrees with any other page, the other page holds.

**Archived ideas** keeps the old essence system of magic, which the current design replaced with eight [[emotion|emotions]] and three sources (see [[Magic]]). Its tables are kept as they were, uncorrected.

## What survives

The vision keeps almost nothing of it:

- **Sleep magic** may find a home in [[Unmaking]], provisionally (see the open question below).
- **Mnemosyne and Lethe** survive only as names, among the Greek names of the oldest, cosmic things (see [[Greek names]]).
- **Time ratio** survives only as an idea: each opposed pair governs one aspect of time (see [[Other ideas]]).

See the open question on [[Unmaking#issue-123|Unmaking]].

## Names that now mean something else

Several archived names collide with current terms. In the archive they mean only what the tables below say.

- The compound **Reverie** (Lethe and Aether) is not a [[Reverie]], a real dream made into a place.
- The compounds **Grief** and **Despair** are not the [[Emotion#Grief|Grief]] and [[Emotion#Despair|Despair]] emotions, and the compound **Nightmare** is not the Nightmares of the Vivid.
- The essences **Memory**, **Fear**, **Wonder** and **Sleep** are not [[Memory magic]], the [[Emotion#Fear|Fear]] emotion, [[Wonder]] or the world's [[sleep cycle]].
- The attunement **Coherence** ("the gap between your Memory ... and your Sleep ... When they meet you go under, and collapsing again makes you an Echo") is not [[Coherence]], and [[going under]] is now something you unlock.
- The value **Capacity** ("how much essence you can hold") is not [[capacity]].
