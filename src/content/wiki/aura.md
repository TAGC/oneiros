An **aura** is the only self-centred area [[delivery]]: [[Held and manifest#Shaped feeling|shaped feeling]] that a will holds around itself. There is no separate shield delivery; shields are auras. Auras are projected, unlike armour, which is physical and worn.

The emotion's [[adverb]] seeps into the area: whatever happens inside takes on its manner, for friend and foe.

## Shape

[[Intensity]] raises both an aura's radius and its strength. Outward [[Emotion|emotions]] grow more in area, inward ones more in intensity.

Every aura tapers from the centre to the rim, and each emotion has its own steepness. Love's taper is the flattest and Hate's the steepest.

## Leak

An aura is a leaky store. [[Channelling]] feeds [[force]] in, and leakage bleeds it out as [[ambient emotion]]. The leak rate varies by emotion. An aura you channel recovers from damage while you channel it.

## Rhythm

Courage's aura arrives in a drumbeat (see [[#Courage]]). Whether other auras have a rhythm is open.

## The eight auras

| Aura    | Grows mostly in                             | Taper                    | Leak    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------- |
| Love    | Area                                        | Flattest                 | High    |
| Hate    | Intensity; hugs close                       | Steepest                 | Low     |
| Joy     | Area, most of all                           | Bright core, thin fringe | Highest |
| Grief   | Intensity, but wide: the widest inward aura | Gentle                   | Low     |
| Courage | Area and intensity evenly                   | Steady                   | Medium  |
| Fear    | Intensity; small                            | Flickering edge          | High    |
| Hope    | Area and reach; wide but faint              | Gentle                   | Least   |
| Despair | Intensity; tight                            | Steep                    | Medium  |

### Love

A Love aura raises [[Harmony]] for all inside. They become more tangible to the whole world: they hit harder and get hit harder, physical things touch them more, and help reaches them more easily. Its adverb is _wholly_. It works as support over allies and as an offensive mark when cast over enemies.

Pressing on a Hate aura, it annihilates at the edge into [[lucidity]], wearing the wall down.

### Hate

A Hate aura lowers Harmony within. It splits the inside of the aura off from the outside: those inside lose Harmony only with the outside, and stay in Harmony with each other. Whoever is inside, friend or foe, is sealed in together, as in a duel.

### Joy

Its adverb is _widely_: single effects inside splash. Joy is contagious: whatever catches Joy passes some to its neighbours, weaker at each step, so it spreads past the aura's edge and dies out a few steps beyond. Joy fire leaps between fuel (see [[Kind]]).

A Joy aura annihilates Grief [[ambient emotion]], which may start lucidity cascades. When an ally's Joy aura overlaps it, both expand.

### Grief

Its adverb is _slowly_: incoming bolts crawl, and damage lands soft and lingers. Things inside grow heavy: doors stick, and all inside move slower, the caster included. Rain falls, and fires smoulder and smoke. Joy annihilates it and cancels the lingering damage.

### Courage

A Courage aura is an anchor: everything inside resists being pushed, both physically and in mood. It resists all mood change, friend or foe, but not the Courage flowing into you.

Courage waves out in steady beats, like a drum at a fixed tempo. Higher intensity makes the beats stronger, not faster. Things inside react in pace with the beat. On the beat, it pushes attackers back, and the push does [[Coherence]] damage. Fear attacks annihilate on contact.

### Fear

When it is hit, a Fear aura clenches: it locks up, very hard to break, but you cannot cast or move much. Creatures inside flinch and miss, both ways. Its adverb is _fleetingly_: effects inside are brief and cheap. Fire, water and loose objects shrink away from the centre, and the air turns to still, oppressive fog. Courage annihilates it.

### Hope

Hope's core is reach. Its adverb is _far_: spells inside fly further, beams stretch, and allies' auras extend. Lanterns inside throw their light further without getting brighter. Hope also lifts: things inside lighten, heavy things are easier to move, and water rises as mist.

Hope leaks least, so a channelled Hope aura ripens over time: it holds what you give it. When it breaks, its stored force spills as ambient Hope. Despair breaks a ripe Hope aura with a large flash of lucidity.

A Hope aura gives no sight, recovery or healing, and does not make doors lead somewhere new.

### Despair

Missiles lose lift and drop at its edge. Its adverb is _suddenly_: channelled spells snap, on both sides. Walls crumble, doors fall, fires die and light goes. Creatures inside slow, and only wills whose qualities lean to Despair (Despondency, Baseness, Obduracy) stop acting (see [[Quality of the will]]).
