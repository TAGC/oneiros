**Uprooting** is dislodging a rooted [[memory]] or [[belief]]: surgery on the psyche. Anything rooted can still be dislodged. It is done by a [[practitioner]], or roughly by yourself through [[Unmaking]]. It is always possible, never easy, and costly for a while.

## Scars

- **Below [[Megaron]]**, a clean uprooting leaves no mark and a botched one leaves a [[scar]].
- **A Megaron** always scars when uprooted, even cleanly, and a memory Megaron also marks the body.

## Why uproot

- **To move a memory out.** Only Loose memories and Fixtures go into [[bead|beads]], so a Room or Megaron must be uprooted before [[recounting]].
- **To clear a collapse.** A collapsed room can be rooted again or uprooted (see [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]).
- **To undo a mutation.** A surfaced memory is undone by uprooting (see [[surfacing]]).
- **To help someone.** Some quests take you into another character's [[Temenos]] to help them uproot a troubling memory. If they let you keep it, it arrives vivid but Loose (see [[Other minds]]).

Like any form that dissolves, an uprooted form keeps its force, which hangs where it happened as [[ambient emotion]]; inside the Temenos it stays in the Temenos.

## Practitioners

Practitioners enter others' houses with permission as a trade, to uproot memories and tend scars, and some turn intruder (see [[Other minds]]). In someone else's house, taking anything rooted would mean uprooting it in place, so [[extraction]] deals only in Loose memories and beliefs at Notion.
