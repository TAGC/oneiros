A **marvel** is a place or thing near which [[Calm]] can become [[Wonder]]. Be calm near a marvel and you pass into [[Stillness]]; when it passes, [[Attention]] flares fade, once per marvel per [[sleep cycle|cycle]], and the place shows its true nature. [[Wonder]] owns these rules.

Insights at marvels are also one source of [[Musing|Musings]], alongside rumours, books, teachings and your [[inner voices]] (see [[Belief stages]]).
