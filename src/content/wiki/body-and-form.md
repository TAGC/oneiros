The body, like everything in [[Dreamlands|the Dreamlands]], is a [[will in motion]]. Getting hurt **changes you rather than just weakening you**: pain can pinch you towards Fear or Grief.

The exterior of your [[Temenos]] is your body; the interior is your mind.

## Getting hurt

[[Coherence|Damage]] lowers your Coherence. It also reshapes your mood, since pain can pinch you towards Fear or Grief (see [[Mood]]).

> [!ISSUE 28]

## How form changes

- **[[Stability]]** sets how much an entity's form shifts with its feelings, from very low for beasts and Nightmares to high for anchored places and old beings.
- **[[Flicker|Flickers]]** are brief changes of form under a strong mood while a memory sits at Room. They are not mutations.
- **[[Mutation|Mutations]]** are lasting changes to your form, and only memories make them: a memory's roots break through to the exterior when it surfaces (see [[Surfacing]]).
- **[[Eidolon|Eidola]]** are the brief form of a memory's mutation, shown while you are [[Ablaze]].

Beliefs never change your form; they change what is true (see [[Belief]]). In low-stability origins such as Nightmare-born, your [[temperament]] shows in the body itself. The [[spinning top]] steadies your form, so lucidity and Forgetting wear on you less.

## Equipment

Equipment uses body-part slots with a **core** layer (affects you) and a **persona** layer (how others see you). See [[Core and persona]]. Gear has stability too, so it reshapes, slips off or binds the change when you flicker.

## Inner voices

Your emotions speak to you as [[Inner voices|inner voices]] in the event log, each in its own rhythm.
