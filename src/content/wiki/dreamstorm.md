A **dreamstorm** is a rare, violent event in the [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]] that reshapes whole places.

Dreamstorms are one of the ways [[Memory|memories]] climb the [[rootedness ladder]]: memories climb by being relived at high-stakes moments, by going under, in dreamstorms and through saturation.

Storms that shape a world's rhythm are drawn from The Stormlight Archive (see [[Inspirations]]).
