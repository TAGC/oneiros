You play a **Lucid**: someone who knows the world is a dream. There is no set story; each world is a new dream of [[The Dreamer|the Dreamer]], and its history follows from that dream.

Your character is the only Lucid in your copy of the world. Not to be confused with [[lucidity]], what opposites annihilate into.

## The spinning top

Your first belief, _this is a dream_, is the [[spinning top]] at the centre of your [[Temenos]], and it is what makes you Lucid. It stands at Dogma from the start and nothing can shake it.

## Acting like everyone else

Knowing the world is a dream is not overt in itself, so a Lucid who acts like everyone else shows the Dreamer nothing (see [[Attention]]).

## Everyone else

The common belief is that the world is real and minds mirror it as a microcosm; a few see it the other way round, but none with the conviction that would make them Lucid. For now, only you know of the Dreamer.
