An **emotion** is one of eight motions of the will (see [[will in motion]]), in four opposed pairs. Pleasant emotions move outward, unpleasant ones inward.

Players see emotions; the motions show in behaviour, animation, sound and the [[ring]] beneath every character and thing, which shows the shape of its [[mood]].

## The eight emotions

| Pair              | Outward                                 | Inward                               |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Joy and Grief     | **Joy:** bursts out                     | **Grief:** large, slow waves in      |
| Courage and Fear  | **Courage:** steady short waves out     | **Fear:** trembling in               |
| Hope and Despair  | **Hope:** one light lift out            | **Despair:** one sudden drop in      |
| Love and Hate     | **Love:** a violent surge out           | **Hate:** the hardest rush in        |

[[Calm]] is the will circling at rest. [[Stillness]] is the will feeling motionless: its force is intact and still moving, but its motion is no longer felt.

Outward feelings spread wide to neighbours; inward ones strike one target deep (see [[Grain]]). Each emotion also makes its own kind of [[aura]] (see [[Aura#The eight auras]]).

### Joy

Bursts out. Its colour is gold, and as an [[adverb]] it acts _widely_. In the [[Temenos]] it has the upper floor of the east wing, and it is felt there as sparks of light.

### Grief

Large, slow waves in. Its colour is azure, and as an adverb it acts _slowly_. It has the cellar of the east wing, and it is felt there as rain.

### Courage

Steady short waves out. Its colour is red, and as an adverb it acts _steadily_. It has the upper floor of the north wing.

### Fear

Trembling in. Its colour is cyan, and as an adverb it acts _fleetingly_. It has the cellar of the north wing, and it is felt there as shivering draughts. Its [[Inner voices|inner voice]] stammers, and in a [[rumour]] it exaggerates the danger.

### Hope

One light lift out. Its colour is spring yellow-green, and as an adverb it acts _far_. It has the upper floor of the south wing. In a rumour it promises rescue.

### Despair

One sudden drop in. Its colour is violet, and as an adverb it acts _suddenly_. It has the cellar of the south wing. In a rumour it predicts doom.

### Love

A violent surge out. Its colour is rose, and as an adverb it acts _wholly_. It has the upper floor of the west wing, and it is felt there as warmth. In a rumour it adds treasure and allies.

### Hate

The hardest rush in. Its colour is green, and as an adverb it acts _deeply_. It has the cellar of the west wing. Its inner voice clips its words, and in a rumour it assigns blame.

## Colours

Each emotion has a colour of light, and opposites are complementary, so together they make white: cancellation made visible.

| Pair             | Outward               | Inward        |
| ---------------- | --------------------- | ------------- |
| Courage and Fear | Red                   | Cyan          |
| Joy and Grief    | Gold                  | Azure         |
| Hope and Despair | Spring yellow-green   | Violet        |
| Love and Hate    | Rose                  | Green         |

Outward emotions are warm and inward ones cool; [[Calm]] is soft white, and [[lucidity]] a brilliant white flash. Mixtures blend, and the same colours hold in [[the Dreamlands]] and in your [[inner world]]. Memories shed [[memory light]] in their emotions' colour.

## Opposites

Wherever opposites meet out in the shared world, they annihilate into [[lucidity]]. Held feeling never annihilates: in any mood, memory or belief, opposites coexist, so you can love and hate at once, and so can a door (see [[Held and manifest]]).
