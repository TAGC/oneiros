A **waking boon** is a benefit granted each [[rest]] by a tended room in your [[Temenos]].

A memory or belief gains a waking boon when it reaches [[Rootedness ladder#Room|Room]]: its effect there is "strong and lasting, plus a waking boon". A collapsed room loses its boons (see [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]).

While a memory is [[Ablaze]], it shows effects it would have at a higher tier, which the vision lists as including its waking boon; the designer has not confirmed that list (see [[Ablaze]]).

> [!OPEN]
> The vision does not yet say what a waking boon does, or how the tended room is chosen.
