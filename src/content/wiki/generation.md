**Generation** is how the game grows itself: an LLM fills gaps in the world, always within the engine's rules. "Grows itself" is one of the five pillars of [[Oneiros]]. The LLM adds tagged triggers, effects and places, checked against the rules. It never writes code.

## The world comes first

As in Dwarf Fortress, [[the Dreamer]] dreams a whole world into existence and its history is simulated. There is no set story; each world is a new dream of the Dreamer, and its history follows from that dream. The generated world is built first, [[Reverie|Reveries]] after (see [[Dream journal]]).

> [!ISSUE 54]

## Within the rules

What the LLM adds is written in the shared effects grammar and costed by the power budget, so generated content stays balanced (see [[Crafting]]).

## Paid in apeiron

[[Apeiron]] pays for LLM generation: new places, people and conversations. Free players see a dry pool, and generative options simply don't appear. Failed generations are refunded.

## Ideas

These are liked but not yet worked through (see [[Other ideas]]):

- World generation builds a tree of splits: each split divides a [[shard]] along its strongest tension into an outward sibling and an inward one, and [[force]] weakens with every generation (see [[Cosmology]]).
- Composition drives visuals, and the LLM writes creature recipes. Image models only for rare art, generated once and cached.
