A **gateway** is a free-standing marble gateway at the white centre of your [[Temenos]], leading to one of your [[Reverie|Reveries]]. The gateways ring the [[apeiron pool]], and each is fed by a [[euripus]].

## A new gateway

When you spend [[apeiron]] to offer a [[Dream journal|dream journal]] entry to [[The Dreamer|the Dreamer]], it becomes a Reverie: the original entry is crystallised as its unchangeable core, and a new gateway to it appears in your Temenos.

## Growing a Reverie

A Reverie extends as you explore it, around what you do, or while you're away if you let apeiron run to it. The euripus from the pool to each gateway sets how much runs: dry, a trickle or running (see [[Euripus]]).
