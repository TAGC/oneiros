An **obol** is a small [[bead]] holding a city's founding [[memory]], and the coin of everyday trade. A coin is only as good as the faith behind it, and a city's obols show its [[Coherence]]: how well its people still move as one.

## The rite

Citizens take part in a deliberately Calm, unchanging rite of the founding, then give the memory up at the temple as a tax or for wages, so they never remember the rite, only that they went. Supply is limited by population and how often the rite runs.

Calm gives the memory no feeling, so an obol is faint, useless for conjuring and worth the same to every buyer. The whole city's [[Conviction]] stands behind it.

## Light

Each obol gives off a small, steady white light: obol light is the steady white kind of [[memory light]]. Cities hang obols as street lamps through the [[Sleep cycle#Vivid|Vivid]], so a city's coin literally keeps it from shifting, since wherever memory light falls things stay [[Remembered]].

Obols are not inherently durable: they last by clustering, their light slowing each other's fading (see [[Memory light#Shelter]]). Like every memory, an obol travels in a bead.

## Value

Anyone can absorb an obol to shore up their own Coherence, which is why everyone accepts them. A city that runs its rites carelessly loses its citizens' Conviction, and its obols wear faster.

How obols sit beside beads and other goods is on [[Economy]].
