**Temperament** is Mainländer's "skin of the will": melancholic, sanguine, choleric or phlegmatic. It sits above the [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]] and shapes which qualities come easily, but never forbids any, since everyone carries the seed of every quality.

It is the second step of [[character creation]], after [[origin]] and before [[backstory]].

## In the body

In low-stability origins such as Nightmare-born, temperament shows in the body itself (see [[stability]]).
