A **guise** is the likeness of someone a target remembers, worn inside their [[inner world]], with your [[mood]] matched to how they feel about that person. It is not the persona equipment layer itself (see [[Core and persona]]), though persona gear can support one.

## In another's mind

Lit areas of a target's house are where their memories shine, and there you are seen, so you must play a part. In the light you wear a guise. Any guise can go anywhere, but it fits some places better than others. You switch guise in the dark, where suspicious actions belong.

## Preparation

How faithful a guise is depends on preparation: what you know of that person, and memories of them, including ones taken by [[extraction]] from others. A heist builds guises in stages, starting with loosely guarded memories and using what you take to build guises for deeper, more tightly guarded rooms (see [[Other minds#Preparation]]).

## In the Dreamlands

Guises also serve subterfuge in the shared world, in two kinds:

- **An illusion:** a [[conjuration]] from a [[bead]] holding a memory of the person. Anyone can make one, but [[lucidity]] breaks it, and it drains your mood in the memory's own emotions while you wear it (see [[Illusion]]).
- **A change of your own form:** governed by [[stability]], it is harder to break and comes easiest to the Nightmare-born (see [[Origin]]).

Persona gear can support either.
