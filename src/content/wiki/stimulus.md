A **stimulus** is a change in the shape of a will's [[mood]] caused by something it meets (a wolf's snarl, a grieving town), after Schopenhauer. It moves no [[force]]: a wolf's snarl pinches you towards Fear, and neither of you gains or loses force.

A stimulus can pinch no further than capacity allows. Once you are as afraid as you can be, it makes you no more afraid (see [[mood capacity]]).

## Stimulus and spells

A [[Raw magic|Raw]] spell is different. It carries your force, and when it lands it grows the target's circle and pinches it its way. Casting spends area from the lobe of the emotion you spend, so the pinch and your total both shrink.

Force from a spell that lands is the target's to spend freely: what you send, they carry (see [[Force]]).

## Motives

A [[motive]] also reshapes a mood without moving force, but through what a will knows rather than what it meets.

## Pain

Getting hurt can pinch you towards Fear or Grief. Whether pain is only a stimulus is open on [[Body and form]].
