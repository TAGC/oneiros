An **outburst** is what happens to [[force]] above your [[mood capacity]] that you have not spent. It comes when you stop being [[Ablaze]], including when [[Burning|burning]] another memory replaces the burn, and when a spell lands on you with more than you have room for. It bursts out of your body all at once: uncontrolled, with no target and no [[adverb]]. Once out it is [[ambient emotion]]: it hangs around you and lands in no will. After a burn it is flavoured by the memory. It is overt, so it flares [[Attention]].

See the open question on [[Attention#issue-182|Attention]].

Only the part above your mood capacity bursts out. What fits stays in your [[mood]]: burnt [[material]] still within mood capacity is not part of the outburst.

## Harm

An outburst costs you [[Coherence]], whether it follows a burn or a spell. This harm is separate from [[Quality of the will#Resistance|resistance]], which your qualities set.

> [!ISSUE 181]

## Avoiding it

- **After a burn.** Spend the burnt material while you are Ablaze. If nothing is left above your mood capacity when the burn ends, there is no outburst.
- **When a spell would overfill you.** [[Redirecting|Redirect]] it, which costs you nothing. Or, with great skill, take the excess in and spend it at once on a spell of your own; that costs Coherence too, but less is left to burst out.

Outbursts are one of the things that help stop a harvest-and-burn loop; see [[Burning#Harvest and burn|harvest and burn]].

See the open question on [[Adverb#issue-136|Adverb]].
