You play a [[Lucid]]: someone who knows the world is a dream. There is no set story; each world is a new dream of [[The Dreamer|the Dreamer]], and its history follows from that dream.

**Character creation** is [[origin]] (Memory-born or Nightmare-born), then [[temperament]], then [[backstory]].

## The three steps

1. **[[Origin]].** Memory-born, the default, or Nightmare-born, the unstable, mutant origin. Your origin sets how stable your form is (see [[stability]]).
2. **[[Temperament]].** Melancholic, sanguine, choleric or phlegmatic: the "skin of the will", which shapes which [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]] come easily. In low-stability origins such as Nightmare-born, temperament shows in the body itself.
3. **[[Backstory]].** Your generated starting memories, precious and finite.

Your character is defined by its qualities of the will, which decide which emotions come most naturally to you: where your [[mood]] rests, and which way it drifts.

## What you start with

- **A surfaced memory.** At creation you pick one of three backstory memories to start surfaced (see [[Surfacing]] and [[Mutation]]).
- **A transformed room**, if you are Nightmare-born.
- **A room on every floor.** Every backstory holds memories of all eight [[Emotion|emotions]], so every floor of your [[Temenos]] starts with at least one room.
- **The spinning top.** Your first belief, _this is a dream_, stands at Dogma from the start and is what makes you Lucid (see [[Spinning top]]).
