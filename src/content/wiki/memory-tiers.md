The **memory tiers** are the names a [[memory]] takes as its [[Hold]] grows, one for each tier of the [[rootedness ladder]]: **Impression**, **Recollection**, **Refrain** and **Touchstone**. [[Belief|Beliefs]] climb the same tiers under their own names, the [[belief stages]].

| Tier                                | Memory       | In the Temenos                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Rootedness ladder#Loose\|Loose]]   | Impression   | An oil lamp on a shelf                             |
| [[Rootedness ladder#Fixture\|Fixture]] | Recollection | Built into a room: a hearth                        |
| [[Rootedness ladder#Room\|Room]]     | Refrain      | A room of its own                                  |
| [[Megaron]]                         | Touchstone   | A load-bearing great hall                          |

## Each tier

- An **Impression** is Loose. Its effect is small, and it is the only kind of memory you can pour into a [[bead]] (see [[recounting]]). Memories from others arrive vivid but Loose, which makes them the readiest fuel for [[burning]].
- A **Recollection** is a Fixture. Its effect is steadier, and burning one means dismantling it in the act of [[igniting]].
- A **Refrain** has a room of its own. Its effect is strong and lasting, plus a [[waking boon]], and its [[mutation]] is shown in full, so a strong mood can already cause brief [[Flicker|flickers]].
- A **Touchstone** is a [[Megaron]]. It may [[surfacing|surface]] as a mutation, and destroying or uprooting it always leaves a [[scar]] on the house and a mark on the body.

## Climbing and falling

Memories climb by being relived at high-stakes moments, by going under, in dreamstorms and through saturation; [[Rest#Long rest|long rests]] also raise Hold. They rise smoothly and fall hard (see [[Rootedness ladder#Rising and falling|rising and falling]] and [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]).

A memory is a snapshot: its composition never changes, and it can only fade or root deeper.
