Your **origin** is the first step of [[character creation]], before [[temperament]] and [[backstory]]. There are two: **Memory-born**, the default, and **Nightmare-born**, the unstable, mutant origin.

## Memory-born

Memory-born is the default origin, with medium [[stability]], like most people.

## Nightmare-born

Nightmare-born is the unstable, mutant origin, with low stability. Nightmare-born characters start with a transformed room. In low-stability origins such as Nightmare-born, temperament shows in the body itself. A guise made by changing your own form, rather than by an illusion, comes easiest to the Nightmare-born (see [[Guise]]).
