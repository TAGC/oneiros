A **motive** is a change in the shape of a will's [[mood]] through what it knows, after Schopenhauer. Its [[Belief|beliefs]] and [[Memory|memories]] shape how a [[stimulus]] or event moves it, and its [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]] decide which reaction arises. No [[force]] moves.

Hit by a hostile Joy spell, you are filled with Joy, and knowing you are attacked may pinch you towards Hate as well. Both are held, so they coexist (see [[Held and manifest]]).
