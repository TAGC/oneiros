**Intensity** is how strong an [[emotion]] is, from 1 to 5. In a spell it is set by the repeats in a [[chain]]: `Hate Hate` reads as Deep Hatred. Intensity has its own words, used for spells, [[Memory|memories]], places and creatures.

## Intensity words

When a spell is read, each verb run collapses into one intensity word (see [[Casting#Reading a spell|reading a spell]]). Level 1 is always the emotion's own name as a noun, so Hate's is Hatred; each level 2 has its own adjective.

| Emotion | 1       | 2             | 3            | 4             | 5          |
| ------- | ------- | ------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ---------- |
| Joy     | Joy     | Great Joy     | Elation      | Ecstasy       | Rapture    |
| Courage | Courage | Firm Courage  | Valour       | Dauntlessness | Heroism    |
| Hope    | Hope    | High Hope     | Faith        | Fervent Faith | Exaltation |
| Love    | Love    | Fierce Love   | Devotion     | Adoration     | Worship    |
| Grief   | Grief   | Heavy Grief   | Mourning     | Anguish       | Desolation |
| Fear    | Fear    | Cold Fear     | Dread        | Terror        | Horror     |
| Despair | Despair | Black Despair | Hopelessness | Abjection     | Ruin       |
| Hate    | Hatred  | Deep Hatred   | Loathing     | Abhorrence    | Execration |

## Limits

Qualities cap how intense a spell can be; [[mood capacity]] caps how much of an emotion you have to spend (see [[Mood]]). High intensity needs deep [[Quality of the will|qualities]] feeding that emotion. The adverb _suddenly_ raises this cap, at a loss (see [[Adverb#Suddenly]]).

The top intensities cost more than a developed character's mood capacity, so they need a source outside what you hold: [[burning]] a memory, which leaves you [[Ablaze]], or a spell from someone else, taken in and spent before it bursts out (see [[Mood capacity#Above the cap|above the cap]]). Part of how the vision describes burning (that it lets you hold more than your mood capacity) is not yet confirmed by the designer; see [[Burning]].
