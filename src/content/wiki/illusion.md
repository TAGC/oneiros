An **illusion** is a flimsy [[conjuration]] from a faint, low-[[vividness]] [[memory]]. Illusions fade by the next [[sleep cycle]] and are broken by [[lucidity]].

> [!OPEN]
> That illusions come from faint, low-vividness memories was added as a knock-on change while the vision was being recorded. The designer has not confirmed it.

## Broken by lucidity

Lucidity dissolves forms in proportion to how weakly they are held (Coherence, stability, being Remembered): illusions first. What it dissolves releases its force as [[ambient emotion]].

## Illusions as guises

In the [[Dreamlands]], an illusion is one of two kinds of [[guise]]: a conjuration from a [[bead]] holding a memory of the person, which anyone can make but lucidity breaks, and which drains your [[mood]] in the memory's own emotions while you wear it. The other kind is a change of your own form, governed by [[stability]], which is harder to break. Persona gear can support either (see [[core and persona]]).
