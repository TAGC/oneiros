**Potions** are charged liquids (water, wine or resin holding a feeling), not health bars in a bottle. [[Rest]] restores [[Coherence]], as do food and potions.

## Brewing

Brew potions at rest, with care for a clean result. Casting on a carried [[vessel]] in the field mostly affects the container, and the contents take a weak, unpredictable charge.

Bottling is one of the crafts, and potions use the same effects grammar and power budget as everything else that is crafted or generated (see [[Crafting]]).
