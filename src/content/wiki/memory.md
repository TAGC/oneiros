A **memory** is a generated item with an emotional composition, a [[vividness]] and a [[Hold]] (how rooted it is). A memory is a snapshot: its composition never changes, though it can fade or root deeper.

Memories and [[Belief|beliefs]] share one rule: each is a form plus amounts of emotional [[material]], in the same units spells use (see [[strength]]). Memories change what you are; beliefs change what is true. Only memories make [[Mutation|mutations]].

## Vividness and Hold

A memory's [[vividness]] comes from novelty and feeling together: an experience without feeling fades, and so does a feeling you have had many times. Vividness sets a [[Conjuration|conjuration's]] detail and how long it lasts, and it falls constantly as the memory sheds [[memory light]].

Its [[Hold]] is how rooted it is. Hold sets its tier on the [[rootedness ladder]], and so its form in the Temenos: the memory tiers are Impression ([[Loose]]), Recollection ([[Fixture]]), Refrain ([[Rootedness ladder|Room]]) and Touchstone ([[Megaron]]). See [[memory tiers]].

Your own vivid memories are usually deeply rooted, so they are hard to [[Burning|burn]]. Memories from others (bought, traded, extracted, or freed when you help someone [[Uprooting|uproot]] a traumatic memory and they let you keep it) arrive vivid but Loose, which makes them the readiest fuel for powerful magic.

## Where memories come from

Memories come from four sources:

- your generated **[[backstory]]** (precious and finite);
- **trading** (second-hand, with a trace of their owner);
- **living** in the Dreamlands (consolidated during [[Rest|long rests]], or they fade);
- **finding** them (planted by others, dropped, or drifting in the deep).

## Where a memory lives

A memory is intangible. Held, it lives in your [[Temenos]]; in the Dreamlands it lives in a [[bead]]. Every memory travels in a bead, obols included. You move a memory between your mind and a bead by [[recounting]] and [[reliving]].

Inside the Temenos, memories appear as oil lamps whose flames burn in their emotion's colour. A memory sits on the floor of its strongest emotion, tugged by the others, and keeps the colour it was felt in. Brightness shows how fast it is fading: a fading memory gutters and a lost one goes out (see [[memory light]]).

## Memory as value

Everything wants to live, and memory is what holds a being together, so memory is the root of all value. Everyday trade uses [[Obol|obols]], small beads holding a city's founding memory; personal beads are goods priced in obols. See [[Economy]].

## Losing memories

[[Forgetting]] is the dark: a constant erosion of memory that surges during [[Sleep cycle|the Vivid]]. Memory loss as such is rare: severe head injuries, forgetting magic, collapsing. A memory can also crack and spill out as [[ambient emotion]] (see [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]]), be [[Burning|burnt]], [[Planting|planted]] in a place or taken by [[extraction]].

## Merging repeated experiences

The vision says repeated experiences merge into one faint but well-rooted memory, so grinding never pays.
