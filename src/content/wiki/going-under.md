**Going under** is an unlockable alternative to becoming an [[Echo]] at zero [[Coherence]]: dropping into a deeper layer of the dream for a second chance. It is something you unlock, for example through a belief such as _death is only a deeper dream_.

Going under is also one of the ways memories climb the [[Rootedness ladder|rootedness ladder]], beside being relived at high-stakes moments, [[Dreamstorm|dreamstorms]] and saturation.
