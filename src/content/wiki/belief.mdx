A **belief** is what you hold true about yourself or the world, and all knowledge is beliefs: a city's capital, when the ferry runs, every [[rumour]] you hear. Most are light and do almost nothing. The ones that matter change the rules: believe falls can't hurt you and they don't; believe you can fly and you can.

Beliefs need not be true. Places, factions and cultures hold beliefs of their own.

## What beliefs change

Memories change what you are; beliefs change what is true. Beliefs about the world apply to anything you perceive, at any distance. A belief about yourself held as a Dogma permanently changes the rules about you, though never your form.

Beliefs never mutate you: only memories make [[Mutation|mutations]]. A belief's rule becomes active at Tenet and permanent at Dogma, and you steer it by [[dwelling]] or doubting. Its effect is hinted at first and shown in full once it binds (see [[Belief stages]]).

## Strength

Memories and beliefs share one rule (see [[Strength]]). A belief is a form plus amounts of emotional [[material]], and its strength has two halves: how much (its own material) and how rooted (its [[Conviction]]). Conviction sets its tier on the [[Rootedness ladder|rootedness ladder]], which for beliefs runs Notion, Tenet, Certainty and Dogma.

Its own material comes from [[dwelling]], up to its [[belief capacity]]. Only that own material counts towards [[mood capacity]] and [[Coherence]]. The size of its effect is its [[weight]]: the material of its [[grounds]] plus its own.

## Colour

Beliefs are fluid: their colour can shift as they soak up feeling. A belief sits in your [[Temenos]] on the floor of its strongest [[emotion]], tugged by the others. Beliefs can shift colour as their Conviction changes or as they are dwelt on, so they move between visits; memories keep the colour they were felt in. Colourless beliefs are kept in the [[library]] as codices.

## Where beliefs come from

- **Rumours.** Every version of a rumour you hear becomes a belief at Notion, and confirming one raises its Conviction.
- **Musings.** Musings come from rumours, books, teachings, insights at marvels and your Voices (see [[Belief stages#Musing|Musing]]). Books in the world carry ideas, not Conviction: reading one gives a Musing, and Conviction can't be traded.
- **Deductions.** Place beliefs side by side in the library to find [[Deduction|deductions]], which become new beliefs.
- **The spinning top.** Your first belief, _this is a dream_, is the [[spinning top]], which is what makes you [[Lucid]].
- **Other minds.** Taking a belief out of someone's mind is [[extraction]], and putting one in is [[inception]]. Both deal only in beliefs at Notion (see [[Other minds]]).

## Attention

A belief draws the Dreamer's [[Attention]] only when its rule visibly bends the dream. It grows with how far the belief defies the dream's natural law, how overtly, and how strong it is: flying over a city at noon on a heavy, firm belief draws a lot; surviving a fall unseen almost none.

## Voices

Voices are emotions, and beliefs shape what they argue for (Fear with _red things are faster_: "Paint the cart red. We can outrun them."). See [[Inner voices]].

## Losing a belief

Destroying a belief does what destroying its form would, from a leaf that burns without trace to a Dogma's shrine defaced as its hall collapses (see [[Belief stages#Destroying a belief|destroying a belief]]). Losing, trading or renouncing beliefs shrinks both your [[Coherence]] and your [[Temenos]]. Losing a ground lightens every belief that rests on it (see [[grounds]]).
