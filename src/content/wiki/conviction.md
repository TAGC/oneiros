**Conviction** is how rooted a [[belief]] is: the rootedness half of its [[strength]], as [[Hold]] is for a memory. It sets the belief's tier on the [[Rootedness ladder|rootedness ladder]], shown as its [[Belief stages|stage]].

## Raising and lowering

A belief's Conviction rises from [[dwelling]] on it, seeing it seem to work and the company of believers; doubt lowers it. Confirming a version of a [[rumour]] raises its Conviction. As Conviction changes, a belief can shift colour and move to another floor of your [[Temenos]] between visits.

If Conviction slips below its tier's line, the room cracks (see [[Cracking and collapse]]).

## It cannot be traded

Books in the world carry ideas, not Conviction: reading one gives a Musing, and Conviction can't be traded. [[Keepsake|Keepsakes]] carry no Hold or Conviction.

## Shared Conviction

Places, factions and cultures hold beliefs of their own. A whole city's Conviction stands behind its [[Obol|obols]].

## Conviction and Hold

Conviction belongs only to beliefs; a memory never has Conviction of its own.
