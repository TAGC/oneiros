A **kyathos** (plural kyathoi) is the game's unit for all liquids, and the only unit [[apeiron]] is shown in: anything paid is shown only in kyathoi. A [[vessel]]'s size, its [[vessel capacity]], is counted in kyathoi.

The unit names are Greek, like the other names of the oldest, cosmic things (see [[Greek names]]).
