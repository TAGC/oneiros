**Hypnagogia** is the first explorable phase of a [[Rest#Long rest|long rest]], as your character falls asleep. You explore your [[Temenos]] before the fast-forward through sleep.

After the fast-forward, you see what changed in [[hypnopompia]], then wake. Only long rests change the Temenos (see [[Rest]]).
