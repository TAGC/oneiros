**Redirecting** is sending a spell that reaches you on, at its caster or elsewhere, instead of taking it in. It stays the same spell. It never joins your [[mood]], so it costs you no [[Coherence]] and causes no [[outburst]], and a redirected [[bolt]] never lands on you. A will needs no magic of its own to redirect.

## Redirecting and taking in

Redirecting is a different act from taking a spell in. A spell that lands joins your mood, and its [[force]] is yours to spend (see [[Force#Force from a spell|force from a spell]]).

Past your [[mood capacity]], taking a spell in and spending the excess before it bursts out takes great skill, and costs Coherence even if you spend it all. Redirecting costs nothing. Allies may cast at a skilled will on purpose so that it can spend more than it holds: this is one way to pay for the top [[Intensity|intensities]].
