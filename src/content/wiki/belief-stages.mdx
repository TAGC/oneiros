The **belief stages** are the tiers a [[belief]] climbs as its [[Conviction]] grows, matching the rootedness tiers: **Notion**, **Tenet**, **Certainty** and **Dogma**. **Musing** is a state, not a stage.

<ul class="status"><li>Notion</li><li>Tenet</li><li>Certainty</li><li>Dogma</li></ul>

A belief's form in your [[Temenos]] deepens with its stage, and its covers are dyed by its [[emotion]] (marbled when mixed). Its effect is hinted at first and shown in full once it binds. Beliefs rise smoothly and fall hard, like memories (see [[Rootedness ladder]]).

## The four stages

- <IconFrame name="Notion" size="sm" /> **Notion** (Loose): a loose leaf on a shelf, with no side effect.
- <IconFrame name="Tenet" size="sm" /> **Tenet** (Fixture): it binds and becomes active, a codex in a bookcase built into the room.
- <IconFrame name="Certainty" size="sm" /> **Certainty** (Room): a room of its own. Strong and lasting, plus a [[waking boon]].
- <IconFrame name="Dogma" size="sm" /> **Dogma** ([[Megaron]]): strongest; its rule and side effect become permanent. A great hall around a sealed shrine holding a codex bound in bronze boards. A belief about yourself held as a Dogma permanently changes the rules about you, though never your form.

Your first belief, the [[spinning top]], stands at Dogma from the start.

The stage names were decided on 22 November 2026. Notion replaced Hearsay, a working name: the bottom name should show only how rooted a belief is, not where it came from. Certainty replaced Creed, which was easy to confuse with Tenet and sounded churchy. The dwelling state, until then called Notion, became Musing.

## Musing

<IconFrame name="Musing" size="sm" /> While you dwell on a belief, it is a Musing: pages on a reading-room desk, bringing a side effect. Musings come from [[Rumour|rumours]], books, teachings, insights at marvels and your Voices. Each floor of the Temenos has its own reading room, whose desk holds that floor's Musings; colourless Musings sit on the [[library]]'s own desk. At its greatest depth a [[quality of the will]] can propose a Musing. Musings leave no [[scar]].

## Destroying a belief

Destroying a belief does what destroying its form would: a leaf burns without trace, a bookcase torn out leaves marks on the wall, a burnt room scars the building and a Dogma's shrine is defaced as its hall collapses. See [[Scar]] and [[Uprooting]].
