The **belief stages** are the tiers a [[belief]] climbs as its [[Conviction]] grows, matching the rootedness tiers: **Hearsay**, **Tenet**, **Creed** and **Dogma**. **Notion** is a state, not a stage.

<ul class="status"><li>Hearsay</li><li>Tenet</li><li>Creed</li><li>Dogma</li></ul>

A belief's form in your [[Temenos]] deepens with its stage, and its covers are dyed by its [[emotion]] (marbled when mixed). Its effect is hinted at first and shown in full once it binds. Beliefs rise smoothly and fall hard, like memories (see [[Rootedness ladder]]).

## The four stages

- **Hearsay** (Loose): a loose leaf on a shelf. Heard or told, with no side effect.
- **Tenet** (Fixture): it binds and becomes active, a codex in a bookcase built into the room.
- **Creed** (Room): a room of its own. Strong and lasting, plus a [[waking boon]].
- **Dogma** ([[Megaron]]): strongest; its rule and side effect become permanent. A great hall around a sealed shrine holding a codex bound in bronze boards. A belief about yourself held as a Dogma permanently changes the rules about you, though never your form.

Your first belief, the [[spinning top]], stands at Dogma from the start.

## Notion

While you dwell on a belief, it is a Notion: pages on a reading-room desk, bringing a side effect. Notions come from [[Rumour|rumours]], books, teachings, insights at marvels and your Voices. Each floor of the Temenos has its own reading room, whose desk holds that floor's Notions; colourless Notions sit on the [[library]]'s own desk. At its greatest depth a [[quality of the will]] can propose a Notion. Notions leave no [[scar]].

> [!CONFLICT]
> The sheet's Notion row said "Pages on its wing's reading-room desk". The vision says "Each floor has shelves and its own reading room, whose desk holds that floor's Notions", but elsewhere that Notions "sit on the desk of their colour's wing". The table uses the floor until this is settled.

## Destroying a belief

Destroying a belief does what destroying its form would: a leaf burns without trace, a bookcase torn out leaves marks on the wall, a burnt room scars the building and a Dogma's shrine is defaced as its hall collapses. See [[Scar]] and [[Uprooting]].
