The **Temenos** is your inner house: a classical marble house, with any grounds around it such as the garden, standing in your [[inner world]]. **The exterior is your body; the interior is your mind.** Inside, you appear as your core self (see [[Core and persona]]).

Every [[memory]] and [[belief]] you hold lives here, in a form set by how deeply it is rooted (see [[rootedness ladder]]). The house is living: it grows with what you hold and shrinks as you lose it, and [[Forgetting]] creeps in as dark wherever nothing shines.

## Size and Coherence

Your Temenos grows physically with your [[Coherence]]. As memories and beliefs root into rooms the house grows, and losing, trading or renouncing them shrinks both.

> [!CONFLICT]
> The vision says "Your Temenos grows physically with it" (Coherence). The Glossary says the Temenos's "size is your Coherence". The vision itself also says "The house is your Coherence" in its description of the house. The vision's "grows physically with it" is used until this is settled.

## Wings and floors

The house has **four wings**, one per emotion pair, set around the centre in colour-wheel order:

| Wing  | Pair             | Upper floor | Cellar  |
| ----- | ---------------- | ----------- | ------- |
| North | Courage and Fear | Courage     | Fear    |
| East  | Joy and Grief    | Joy         | Grief   |
| South | Hope and Despair | Hope        | Despair |
| West  | Love and Hate    | Love        | Hate    |

Each wing has an **upper floor** for its outward emotion and a **cellar** for its inward one. Where Hope meets Hate and Despair meets Love, **crossing stairs** join the floors.

Memories and beliefs sit on the floor of their strongest emotion, tugged by the others; one split between neighbouring emotions sits on the boundary or the stairs. Beliefs can shift colour as their [[Conviction]] changes or as they are dwelt on, so they move between visits; memories keep the colour they were felt in.

Each floor has shelves and its own **reading room**, whose desk holds that floor's [[Belief stages|Musings]].

> [!OPEN]
> No reason is recorded for which wing faces which direction. Claude found support for Fear in the north and Love in the west, as a suggestion, not a decision. The Tibetan mandala of the five Buddhas puts fearlessness (Amoghasiddhi) in the north, desire (Amitabha, red) in the west and white (Vairocana) in the centre. The Chinese five phases put fear in the north. In one fragment of Alcaeus, the west wind Zephyrus is the father of Eros. Of the eight layouts that keep colour-wheel order, only this one puts Fear north and Love west.

> [!OPEN]
> No reason is recorded for where the crossing stairs are. Claude's suggestion: they sit where the colour wheel wraps round, so Hope's spring yellow-green meets Hate's green, and Despair's violet meets Love's rose.

> [!OPEN]
> Where you enter the Temenos, and which wing faces the entrance, is not decided. In a Roman house the entrance passage leads into the atrium, but the wiki does not say whether the hallway is that passage. Claude suggested putting the Love and Hate wing on the entrance axis, since Mainländer held love and hate to be the strongest motions.

## Plot

The depth of the [[Quality of the will|qualities]] that feed an emotion sets its floor's **plot**, and memories and beliefs of that emotion build rooms on it. Rooms and [[Megaron|Megara]] take up plot. Unbuilt plot shows as foundations or courtyard, and overflow crowds the stairs. Every [[backstory]] holds memories of all eight emotions, so every floor starts with at least one room.

[[Scar|Scars]] and collapsed rooms still take their plot (see [[Cracking and collapse|cracking and collapse]]).

## The centre

**The centre** is white. It holds:

- the [[apeiron pool]], beneath an opening in the roof that lets in light, never rain;
- free-standing marble [[Gateway|gateways]] to your [[Reverie|Reveries]] ringing the pool, each fed by a [[euripus]];
- the [[spinning top]], your first belief (_this is a dream_), which is what makes you [[Lucid]].

Calm fills the central room.

## The hallway

**The hallway** holds doors to [[Anchored place|anchored places]], for fast travel.

## The library

**The [[library]]** rings the centre's upper floor: an annular gallery looking down through the light onto the apeiron pool, the colourless counterpart of the wings. Its doors open onto the four wings' upper floors; the cellars are reached only from below, past the pool.

## Garden and guest rooms

- **The garden** (optional) grows ingredients between rests.
- **Guest rooms** are for companions you have helped.

## Look and atmosphere

The Temenos is a classical marble house: a temple-like exterior of columns and colonnades around the plan of a Hellenistic or Roman house. The white centre is its atrium, and the wings open off a colonnaded peristyle; floors are marble and mosaic, lamps burn oil, and amphorae stand by the pool (see [[Art direction]]).

Emotions are felt as atmosphere: rain for Grief, sparks of light for Joy, warmth for Love, shivering draughts for Fear.

## Forms

- **Memories** appear as oil lamps whose flames burn in their emotion's colour. Brightness shows how fast a memory is fading; a fading memory gutters and a lost one goes out (see [[Memory light]]).
- **Beliefs** take a form that deepens with their stage: a loose leaf on a shelf, pages on a desk, a codex in a bookcase built into the room, a room of their own and finally a great hall around a sealed shrine holding a codex bound in bronze boards. Their covers are dyed by their emotion, marbled when mixed (see [[Belief stages]]).

> [!CONFLICT]
> The sheet's Forms row said "brightness shows vividness". The vision says brightness "shows how fast it is fading", and that brightness "is the rate of that loss". The vision's version is used until this is settled.

## Qualities

[[Quality of the will|Qualities of the will]] style the whole house. For now all styling is cosmetic: it shows who you have become but changes nothing you can do (see [[Quality styles]]).

## Inside the house

- Only long rests change the Temenos; during short rests nothing roots or reorganises (see [[Rest]]).
- You move memories between your mind and a [[bead]] anywhere in the Dreamlands, never inside the Temenos (see [[recounting]]).
- Held memories light only your Temenos. Inside, everything is held, so colour plays no part in how memory light shelters (see [[Memory light]]).
- Force freed by any form that dissolves inside the Temenos stays in the Temenos as [[ambient emotion]].
- Other characters have houses too, which you can enter on quests (see [[Other minds]]).
