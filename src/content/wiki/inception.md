**Inception** is putting a [[memory]] or [[belief]] into someone's mind. It is the opposite of [[extraction]]. Giving a memory to a place instead is [[planting]].

Inception happens on the [[meeting ground]], where your [[Temenos]] and the target's stand side by side. No [[bead]] is involved: memories move directly between the two houses. Inside their house memories are lamps, and you leave one by carrying it, as you would a real lamp; a belief at Notion gives no light, so it can be left entirely in the dark.

An incepted memory arrives Loose and an incepted belief at Notion, and they root or fade through the target's long rests like anything else they hold (see [[Other minds#What moves]]).
