A **composition** is the mix of [[emotion|emotions]] in a spell, [[memory]] or thing.

## In a spell

All magic is an emotional composition plus one emotion used as an [[adverb]]. A spell's composition is any mix of the eight: on the wheel you [[chain]] emotions, and repeats are [[intensity]]. Opposites cancel pair by pair into [[lucidity]] (see [[Casting#Composition|Casting]]).

Each source takes its composition from somewhere different (see [[Magic#Three sources|Magic]]):

| Source           | Composition from         |
| ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| [[Raw magic]]    | The chain you build      |
| [[Memory magic]] | The emotions you feed it |
| [[Unmaking]]     | The feelings to remove   |

Every emotion in the composition pushes on its target at once (see [[Kind]]).

## In a memory

A memory is a snapshot: its composition never changes, though it can fade or root deeper. Its [[memory light]] runs down in proportion to its composition, so the ratio never changes.

## In a place

[[Planting]] a memory shifts the place's composition.
