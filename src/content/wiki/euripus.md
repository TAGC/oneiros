A **euripus** (plural **euripi**) is a short water channel cut into the floor of your [[Temenos]]'s centre, running out from the [[apeiron pool]]'s rim like a spoke to a [[gateway]].

Its bronze sluice sets it **dry**, a **trickle** or **running**. Letting [[apeiron]] run to a gateway lets [[The Dreamer|the Dreamer]] grow that [[Reverie]] while you're away. All growth stays true to the Reverie's core.
