**Magic** in Oneiros is emotional. Everything in [[the Dreamlands]] is a [[will in motion]]: creatures, fire, water, doors, even stone. Feelings are how that motion is felt from inside, so every will has a [[mood]], its current feeling, which comes to rest at its [[grain]].

Magic changes how things feel. A fire given Despair sinks and dies; a door filled with Fear flinches open; a seed given Hope lifts and grows.

## Building a spell

All magic is an emotional [[composition]] plus one [[emotion]] used as an [[adverb]]. How spells are built, priced and read is set out under [[Casting]]; how strong a spell is, under [[Intensity]]; and how each kind of thing responds, under [[Kind]].

## Three sources

| Source             | Does                                                                         | Composition from         | Paid with                                         |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Raw magic\|Raw]] | **Moves:** shifts the mood of existing things                                | The chain you build      | Your mood                                         |
| [[Memory magic\|Memory]] | **Makes:** conjures what you remember; raises Coherence, heals, repairs | The emotions you feed it | Your mood; too much power risks cracking the bead |
| [[Unmaking]]       | **Unmakes:** drains feeling, lowers Coherence, makes things forget           | The feelings to remove   | Your own Coherence                                |

In short: emotions decide how things move; Memory and Unmaking decide how much they exist.

- [[Raw magic]] never conjures: it shifts a thing's mood, and the thing acts it out.
- [[Memory magic]] conjures from a [[bead]]: the memory says what, you say how.
- [[Unmaking]] dissolves form, freeing its feeling into the place as [[ambient emotion]]. It is provisional.

Raw and Memory magic both spend your [[mood]], and you can only spend what you hold. Some powerful magic, Unmaking above all, is paid in your own [[Coherence]], like blood magic.

## Limits

The vision lists, among ideas liked but not yet worked through, that magic follows Sanderson's laws: limits matter more than powers (see [[Other ideas]]).

Whether Unmaking is the right home for the old Sleep magic is open on [[Unmaking]]; the superseded essence system is kept on [[Archived ideas]].
