The **meeting ground** (in lore, _Metaxy_) is a small space where two [[Inner world|inner worlds]] meet, holding your [[Temenos]] and the target's side by side. [[Extraction]] and [[inception]] happen here, and it is how you enter someone's house to help them. No [[bead]] is involved: memories move directly between the two houses.

What you can do once inside, and when best to go in, is set out on [[Other minds]].

## Getting in

You reach the meeting ground during the target's long rest, either with their permission or by opening their mind with [[Raw magic|Raw]] or [[Memory magic]], cast in [[the Dreamlands]] on their sleeping body.

Your own body sleeps nearby and is exposed while you are away; if it is hurt, you wake. Your house is not tended while you infiltrate, so a visit costs you a long rest's benefits.

## Their Attention

Inside, the target plays [[the Dreamer]]'s role in their own world. As a [[Lucid]] who acts like everyone else shows the Dreamer nothing, an intruder who fits in shows the target nothing.

- Acts that don't fit draw their [[Attention]], which leaks away as you go quiet.
- At [[Fixed upon]] the meeting ground collapses and throws you out.
- To them it was a dream: they suspect an intrusion only if you drew enough Attention, and more readily if they believe intrusions are possible.
- A permitted guest draws Attention only by going beyond what they were invited to do.

## The name

_Metaxy_ is the meeting ground's lore name, beside _koinos kosmos_ for the Dreamlands and _idios kosmos_ for an inner world. Greek is used for these only in lore (see [[Greek names]]).
