A belief's **grounds** are what it rests on: the [[Memory|memories]] it rests on (of the people and places it is about, and of paying a cost to act on it) and other [[Belief|beliefs]] one step removed, which lend only their own [[material]]. Only what is in your [[Temenos]] counts. Grounds are attached when the belief forms and by [[dwelling]].

## What grounds set

- **Weight.** A belief's [[weight]] is the material of its grounds plus its own.
- **Affinity.** Its grounds, with your [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]], set which emotions it soaks up when you dwell on it.
- **Belief capacity.** Its grounds set how much it can soak up (see [[belief capacity]]).

"My mother will always forgive me", resting on vivid Love memories, weighs a great deal without defying anything.

## Losing a ground

Losing a ground (selling, [[planting]], [[burning]] or forgetting a memory) lightens every belief that rests on it, and the game warns you first. While a memory is out of you in a [[bead]], beliefs resting on it lighten too.

Memories are grounds for beliefs, while [[Conviction]] feeds Hold; what damps that loop is open (see [[Conviction#Conviction and Hold]]).
