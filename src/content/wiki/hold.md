**Hold** is how rooted a [[memory]] is in a will. It is the rootedness half of the memory's [[strength]], beside its [[material]]; [[Conviction]] is the same half for a [[belief]]. Rootedness sets the tier on the [[rootedness ladder]], and so the kind and permanence of the memory's effect (see [[memory tiers]]).

## What Hold does

- **Fading.** Inside you, a memory loses a fixed fraction of its light at any moment, smaller the higher its Hold. A fresh, Loose memory blazes and dims fast; a deeply rooted one glows softly for a long time (see [[memory light]]).
- **Cracking.** Inside you, the lower a memory's vividness and Hold, the more likely it is to crack and spill out as [[ambient emotion]] (see [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]]).
- **Burning.** How long you stay [[Ablaze]] scales with the memory's Hold alone.
- **Coherence.** Hold sets the tier, and the tier factor multiplies the memory's material into its share of maximum [[Coherence]], so a faint but deeply rooted memory still holds you together.

## Raising Hold

Long rests raise Hold, weighted towards recent memories, so memories fade ever more slowly as they age (Jost's law). In the [[Deep]] phase of a long rest recent memories consolidate, their Hold rising. Memories also climb by being relived at high-stakes moments, by [[going under]], in [[Dreamstorm|dreamstorms]] and through saturation. Memories rise smoothly and fall hard.

The vision also says repeated experiences merge into one faint but well-rooted memory; this is not confirmed (see [[Memory#Merging repeated experiences|merging]]).

Conviction feeds Hold, while memories are [[grounds]] for beliefs; what damps that loop is open (see [[Conviction]]).

> [!CONFLICT]
> The Glossary says Hold "rises during long rests (weighted towards recent memories), with recurrence and with Conviction". The vision names long rests, being relived at high-stakes moments, going under, dreamstorms and saturation, and does not list recurrence. The vision's list is used until this is settled.

## Losing Hold

[[Forgetting]] wears Hold: the Dark [[scar]] malus makes neighbouring rooms lose Hold faster to it. If Hold slips below its tier's line, the room cracks, a warning that mends if it recovers; left too long or too far below, it collapses (see [[cracking and collapse]]).

A memory in a [[bead]] has no Hold. [[Reliving]] a memory brings it back Loose. Igniting a [[Fixture]] carefully keeps its Hold, so it burns long (see [[igniting]]).

> [!OPEN]
> "Crack" has two senses around Hold. A memory cracks when it is weak, spilling out as ambient emotion (see [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]]). A room cracks when Hold slips below its tier's line, as a warning before it collapses (see [[cracking and collapse]]). The Glossary's Hold entry uses both. Whether one should get another word is not settled.
