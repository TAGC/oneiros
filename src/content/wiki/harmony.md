**Harmony** is how tangible a will is to [[the Dreamlands]]. It is continuous, not a switch between states.

## What it changes

Harmony is double-edged.

| Harmony | You take | Physical things | Your own spells |
| ------- | -------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| Low     | Less damage | Interact with you less | Land on others and the world more weakly |
| High    | More damage | Interact with you more | Land on others and the world more strongly |

## What moves it

Hate lowers Harmony; Love raises it. Their [[Aura|auras]] do this to everyone inside them (see [[Aura#Love]] and [[Aura#Hate]]).

## Not stability

Harmony is distinct from [[stability]]. Stability is how much your body shows your [[mood]]; Harmony is how tangible you are.

## Why

Schopenhauer wrote that envy builds the wall between Thee and Me "thicker and stronger", while sympathy thins it, makes it transparent and sometimes pulls it down (_On Human Nature_, tr. T. Bailey Saunders, about p. 7). In the game, Envy, a [[Quality of the will|quality of the will]], feeds Hate, and Good will feeds Love.

The idea is inspired by the phasing in Caves of Qud, made continuous. The idea is borrowed, not the name.
