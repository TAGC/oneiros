An **inner world** is a will's private world, where what it holds lives: its [[mood]], [[Memory|memories]] and [[Belief|beliefs]]. Your [[Temenos]] stands in yours. In lore it is the _idios kosmos_ (see [[Greek names]]).

Its counterpart is the [[Dreamlands]], the shared world. The same colours of feeling hold in both. What is held in an inner world is [[Held and manifest|held]]; its becoming expressed in the Dreamlands is [[manifestation]].

## Where two meet

The [[meeting ground]] (in lore, Metaxy) is a small space where two inner worlds meet, holding your Temenos and another's side by side. There, the target plays the [[Dreamer]]'s role in their own world (see [[Other minds]]).
