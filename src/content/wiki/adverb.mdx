An **adverb** is the [[emotion]] that shapes how a spell acts, written after the separator. A spell is a [[composition]] with any number of adverbs, including none (see [[Casting]]).

## The eight adverbs

| Emotion | Adverb       |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Joy     | _widely_     |
| Courage | _steadily_   |
| Hope    | _far_        |
| Love    | _wholly_     |
| Grief   | _slowly_     |
| Fear    | _fleetingly_ |
| Despair | _suddenly_   |
| Hate    | _deeply_     |

_Fleetingly_ cuts the [[material]] the verb requires, while _steadily_, _wholly_, _far_ and _widely_ raise it. _Suddenly_ is a trade (see [[#Suddenly]]).

## Rules

Any emotion can be an adverb, a verb's opposite as adverb cancels into a [[lucidity]] dispel, and adverbs share the [[chain|chain cap]] with the verbs. The full rules, including chained adverbs, are on [[Casting#Adverbs|Casting]].

## Suddenly

_Suddenly_ (Despair) is a trade:

- **Same price.** Each emotion put in costs what it always does.
- **All at once.** The whole spell goes out in one instant. It cannot be [[Channelling|channelled]], and nothing lingers.
- **No cap.** It lifts the cap on [[intensity]].
- **Lossy past the cap.** Past the normal cap, only part of the excess goes into the spell, and the share falls the further past the cap you go.
- **The spell bursts.** The excess bursts from the spell itself, not from the caster.

It fits the manner "all at once, then over": Despair gives up restraint, and the spell collapses under its own load. The image is duralumin in Mistborn; the idea is borrowed, not the name.
