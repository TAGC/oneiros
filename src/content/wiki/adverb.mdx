An **adverb** is the [[emotion]] that shapes how a spell acts, written after the separator. Every spell is a [[composition]] plus one emotion used as an adverb (see [[Casting]]).

## The eight adverbs

| Emotion | Adverb       |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Joy     | _widely_     |
| Courage | _steadily_   |
| Hope    | _far_        |
| Love    | _wholly_     |
| Grief   | _slowly_     |
| Fear    | _fleetingly_ |
| Despair | _suddenly_   |
| Hate    | _deeply_     |

_Fleetingly_ and _suddenly_ cut the [[material]] the verb requires, while _steadily_, _wholly_, _far_ and _widely_ raise it.

## Rules

Any emotion can be an adverb, a verb's opposite as adverb cancels into a [[lucidity]] dispel, and adverbs share the [[chain|chain cap]] with the verbs. The full rules, including chained adverbs, are on [[Casting#Adverbs|Casting]].
