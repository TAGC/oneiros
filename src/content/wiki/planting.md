**Planting** a [[memory]] gives a piece of yourself to a place: the place takes the [[bead]] in and holds the memory, so its [[Coherence]] rises. Planting always costs you the memory. The opposite is [[gleaning]].

## What planting does

Planting a memory can:

- shift the place's composition;
- anchor fast-travel doors, making it an [[anchored place]];
- make a village remember you;
- start [[Rumour|rumours]];
- leave a message for other players.

Lost in the deep, you can plant one as a [[beacon]] to pull you back up.

## Giving up the memory

Because planting costs you the memory, every [[belief]] resting on it as a [[grounds|ground]] lightens, and the game warns you first. Planting a memory is also one way to stop it [[surfacing]] as a mutation.

## Other players

The Dreamlands are one shared world, played apart: what others leave flows in, planted memories among it. Memories you find may have been planted by others (see [[Memory#Where memories come from|where memories come from]]).
