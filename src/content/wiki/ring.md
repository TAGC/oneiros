The **ring** is the circle beneath every character and thing that shows the shape of its [[mood]]. Its lobes are pinched towards each [[emotion]] in that emotion's colour, the unpinched rest is in [[Calm]]'s soft white, and its size is the will's total [[force]] (see [[Mood#The pinched circle|the pinched circle]]).

The motions of the emotions show in behaviour, animation, sound and the ring.

## When rings appear

Rings appear when you [[inspect]], open the wheel, or see clearly through [[lucidity]] or [[Wonder]]. Lucidity reveals rings.

In [[Stillness]] your ring stills.
