A **vessel** is any container for a liquid, such as a [[Potions|potion]] or [[apeiron]]. How much it holds is its [[vessel capacity]], counted in [[Kyathos|kyathoi]].

## Bound vessel

Fill a bound vessel from the [[apeiron pool]] in your [[Temenos]]; what you carry is your spending cap in the world. Its vessel capacity sets how much apeiron you can spend there. Spilt or stolen apeiron returns to the pool after a [[sleep cycle]], so nothing paid is lost (see [[Apeiron]]).

Bought top-ups of apeiron appear as sealed vessels beside the pool (see [[Apeiron pool]]).

## Potions in a vessel

Casting on a carried vessel in the field mostly affects the container, and the contents take a weak, unpredictable charge. Potions are brewed at rest instead (see [[Potions]]).
