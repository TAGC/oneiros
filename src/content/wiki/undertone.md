An **undertone** is a feeling a thing holds beneath its main feeling. Cancelling it releases [[lucidity]].

[[Inspect]] shows each thing's undertone, alongside its [[kind]] and [[grain]].

A held undertone is one of the places where opposites annihilate into lucidity, yet held feeling otherwise never annihilates. How held undertones fit that rule is open on [[Held and manifest]].
