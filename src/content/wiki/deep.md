The **Deep** is the middle phase of the [[sleep cycle]], about 35% of it: still and heavy, when the deep layers come closer.

## In the house

A house's phases mirror the world's. During a long [[rest]], in the Deep recent memories consolidate, their [[Hold]] rising, weighted towards the most recent.

For someone entering another character's mind, the Deep is the hardest and riskiest time to get in, but the easiest once inside, since Attention climbs slowest (see [[Other minds]]).

## Not "the deep"

The phase is always capitalised. The vision also speaks of a lowercase "deep": memories drifting in the deep, being lost in the deep, and the Dreamer's deep among the [[The Dreamlands#Vertical layers|vertical layers]]. Whether the phase's "deep layers" are that same deep is open (see [[The Dreamlands#Vertical layers]]).
