A **bolt** is a [[delivery]] that sends a spell as a single shot that flies. Its role is range and safety, paid for in route losses and in being dodged or shot down.

## In flight

A bolt crosses the air, so opposed [[ambient emotion]] eats some of it on the way. Opposite bolts that cross annihilate mid-air into [[lucidity]]. A bolt flying through an [[aura]] takes on that aura's [[adverb]]. Its own adverbs shape it.

## On hit

On hit, a bolt joins the target's [[mood]] and does [[Coherence]] damage, scaled by the target's qualities (see [[Quality of the will#Resistance|resistance]]). It does that damage whether or not the target has room for its emotion. A bolt that is [[Redirecting|redirected]] never lands, so it does neither.

## The name

The designer chose "bolt" over missile, projectile, shot, dart, lance and ray. "Orb" is taken by [[Ablaze]], and "spark" belongs to [[lucidity]].
