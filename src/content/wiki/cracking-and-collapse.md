**Cracking and collapse** is what happens to a room in your [[Temenos]] when the thing it holds slips down the [[rootedness ladder]]. If a [[memory]]'s [[Hold]] or a [[belief]]'s [[Conviction]] slips below its tier's line, the room cracks. Left too long or too far below, it collapses.

This page is about rooms. A memory itself can also crack, spilling out as [[ambient emotion]]; that is a different rule, set out under [[Memory light]].

## Cracking

A crack is a warning. It mends if the Hold or Conviction recovers.

Memories and beliefs rise smoothly and fall hard, so a crack is the first sign of a fall (see [[Rootedness ladder#Rising and falling|rising and falling]]).

## Collapse

Left too long or too far below its line, a cracked room collapses. A collapsed room:

- keeps its [[mood capacity]];
- counts only ×0.5 towards [[Coherence]], whatever its tier;
- loses its boons;
- still takes its [[Temenos#Plot|plot]];
- carries a [[scar]]'s maluses;
- buries what it held.

Restore it by rooting it again, or uproot it (see [[uprooting]]).

Collapse is also the destroyed form of a [[Megaron]]: a collapsed hall with sagging neighbours, which always leaves a scar (see [[Scar]]). A [[Belief stages|Dogma]]'s shrine is defaced as its hall collapses.
