**Parked topics** are ideas the designer has flagged to discuss later. Each one is an open question. When one is settled, its outcome goes on its owner page and the topic leaves this page. For ideas the vision keeps for later, see [[Other ideas]].

## Calm as the material of stable things

Parked on 21 November 2026. Anything stable might need to be forged from [[Calm]], and Calm might be the primary material of most things.

The idea arose from [[obol|obols]] being minted in Calm, so their light has no opposite and clusters without sparking [[lucidity]] (see [[Memory light]]).

## Glass is inert to emotion

Parked on 21 November 2026. Glass might be inert to every form of [[emotion]].

The idea arose from [[bead|beads]] being glass-like containers that hold a memory while it is in the Dreamlands. The [[Kind|Kinds]] table has every solid thing react to every emotion, so glass would be an exception that needs a reason. It may tie into the Calm topic above.
