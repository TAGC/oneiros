**Attention** is a will's awareness of a disturbance in its own world. It works as a leaky integrator: it adds up what draws it and always leaks away.

The largest case is the [[Dreamer]]'s Attention to you in the [[Dreamlands]], which works like glimmer in Caves of Qud. It adds up flares and always leaks away over a [[sleep cycle]]. As it rises, hostility and rewards rise together.

## What draws it

**Flares.** Blatant magic and [[lucidity]] draw it as flares. [[Burning]] draws heavy Attention, and an [[outburst]] flares it (both still to be confirmed by the designer; see those pages). A [[beacon]] draws Attention while you hold out in the deep.

**Beliefs.** A [[belief]] draws Attention only when its rule visibly bends the dream. Attention grows with how far the belief defies the dream's natural law, how overtly, and how strong it is: flying over a city at noon on a heavy, firm belief draws a lot; surviving a fall unseen almost none.

Knowing the world is a dream is not overt in itself, so a [[Lucid]] who acts like everyone else shows the Dreamer nothing. A belief whose rule is always overt draws Attention steadily, so any baseline is just where that steady draw balances the leak.

## What clears it

Attention always leaks away over a sleep cycle. [[Wonder]] also clears it: when Stillness passes, Attention flares fade, once per [[marvel]] per cycle.

[[Apeiron]] does not change it: Attention stays dangerous for everyone, and apeiron only decides whether what the Dreamer brings is new or familiar.

## In other minds

Inside another character's mind, the target plays the Dreamer's role in their own world. Acts that don't fit draw their Attention, which leaks away as you go quiet, and at Fixed upon the meeting ground collapses and throws you out. [[Meeting ground]] owns these rules; see also [[Other minds]].
