**Held** and **manifest** say where feeling is. Held feeling is inside a will, in a [[mood]], [[memory]] or [[belief]], whatever the body. Manifest feeling is out in the shared world as stuff.

The difference decides whether opposites annihilate. Held feeling never annihilates; manifest feeling does.

## Held

In any mood, memory or belief, opposites coexist: you can love and hate at once, and so can a door. A spell that lands on a will joins its mood, even alongside its opposite. Once it lands, its [[force]] is the target's to spend freely.

## Manifest

Opposites annihilate into [[lucidity]] only where both are manifest, out in the shared world as stuff:

- two spells meeting (Love against Hate);
- a Joy spell striking Grief armour, which loses integrity as part of it bursts into sparks of lucidity;
- [[Conjuration|conjurations]], which are manifest too;
- [[ambient emotion]], the freed force of a dissolved form.

Force becomes manifest the moment it leaves a mood. A [[chain]] is only a plan until you cast it, and its opposites cancel at the cast.

## Casting opposites at one target

To cast opposites at one target, keep them apart until they land: sequential casts, twin casting or a buffer.

> [!OPEN]
> Twin casting and a buffer are named as ways to keep opposites apart until they land, but neither is designed yet.
