The core game is free. A subscription provides **apeiron**, the formless stuff of dreams carried by the world-river [[Oceanus]], which pays for LLM [[generation]]: new places, people and conversations. It buys novelty, never power. This is the pillar "Fair to play free" (see [[Oneiros]]).

## From pool to world

- **Measured in kyathoi.** Anything paid is shown only in [[Kyathos|kyathoi]], the game's unit for all liquids.
- **Kept in your Temenos pool.** It sits in the [[apeiron pool]] at the centre of your [[Temenos]]. Free players see a dry pool, and generative options simply don't appear.
- **Carried in a vessel.** Fill a bound [[vessel]] from the pool; what you carry is your spending cap in the world.
- **Run to your Reveries.** [[Euripus|Euripi]] from the pool to each [[gateway]] let [[the Dreamer]] grow a [[Reverie]] while you're away: dry, a trickle or running.

Spending apeiron is also how you offer a [[dream journal]] entry to the Dreamer, who turns it into a Reverie.

## Spending

- **Confirmations are optional:** always ask, ask above a set amount (the default), or never ask.
- **Nothing paid is lost.** Spilt or stolen apeiron returns to the pool after a [[sleep cycle]], and failed generations are refunded.
- **Refills.** The subscription tops the pool up each month; bought top-ups never expire. How top-ups stand beside the pool is on [[Apeiron pool]].

## Never convertible

Apeiron can't be harvested, traded, crafted or turned into memories. For what else can never be traded, see [[Economy#What cannot be traded]].

## Attention

[[Attention]] stays dangerous for everyone. Apeiron only decides whether what the Dreamer brings is new or familiar.
