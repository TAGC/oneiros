Everything in [[the Dreamlands]] is a **will in motion**, after Philipp Mainländer: creatures, fire, water, doors, even stone. Feelings are how that motion is felt from inside. So every will has a [[mood]], its current feeling, which comes to rest at its [[grain]]. The body, like everything in the Dreamlands, is a will in motion too.

## Motions

The eight [[emotion|emotions]] are motions of the will. [[Calm]] is the will circling at rest, and [[Stillness]] is the will feeling motionless, though its force still moves.

A will's [[force]] (Kraft) is the amount of its motion: the area of its mood's circle.

## Holding together

[[Coherence]] is how well a will keeps its own particular motion instead of breaking apart into separate, simpler motions. Collective wills such as cities have it too.

## From Mainländer

The vision takes from Mainländer: everything is will in motion; feelings as motions; [[Quality of the will|qualities of the will]]; a world moving towards its end (see [[Inspirations]] and [[Cosmology]]). [[Temperament]] is Mainländer's "skin of the will".
