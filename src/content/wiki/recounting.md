**Recounting** pours a Loose [[memory]] or a [[Fixture]] from your mind into an empty [[bead]]. You can move a memory between your mind and a bead anywhere in the [[Dreamlands]], never inside the [[Temenos]]. A Room or [[Megaron]] must be [[Uprooting|uprooted]] first. The opposite is [[reliving]].

Once recounted, you still know about the memory but no longer feel it: its [[mood capacity]] and share of [[Coherence]] leave with it (see [[Bead#While a memory is out|while a memory is out]]). Conjuring and [[burning]] need a bead in the Dreamlands, so you prepare the memories you want to use, for magic or trade, by recounting them.

## Transfers

These rules hold for both recounting and reliving.

- **Rate.** [[Vividness]] sets how much memory flows. The memory's [[Hold]] and the glass set the rate: the more Hold, the slower. Like a data transfer, vividness is the amount of data, and Hold and the glass set the transfer rate.
- **Concentration.** A transfer works like a D&D concentration spell: one at a time, damage forces a check, being knocked out ends it, and stopping by choice is safe.
- **Interruption.** An interrupted transfer loses the whole memory: its material floods out as [[ambient emotion]] and the bead is left [[dun]].

Otherwise nothing is lost.

## Recounting a Fixture

A Fixture recounted into a bead loses all its Hold, like dismantling a fireplace. For a Fixture, and only a Fixture, you set the rate yourself. Too slow, and you may be interrupted before you finish. Too fast, and you may be [[scar|scarred]]: marks on the wall, where the Fixture was torn out.
