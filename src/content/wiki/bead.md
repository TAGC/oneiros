A **bead** is a glass-like container. A [[memory]] is intangible: held, it lives in your [[Temenos]]; in the [[Dreamlands]] it lives in a bead. An empty bead is [[dun]]. Every memory travels in a bead, [[Obol|obols]] included.

A memory enters a bead through [[recounting]] and leaves it through [[reliving]]. Both happen anywhere in the Dreamlands, never inside the Temenos. Only a Loose memory or a Fixture can be recounted into a bead (a Room or Megaron must be [[Uprooting|uprooted]] first). [[Burning]] destroys both the memory and its bead, whose glass shatters. The bead holds the memory's form, so it can project it in [[conjuration]] (see [[resonance]]).

## While a memory is out

While a memory is in a bead, you know about it but don't feel it: its [[mood capacity]] and share of [[Coherence]] leave with it, and [[Belief|beliefs]] resting on it lighten. Conjuring needs a bead in the Dreamlands, so you prepare the memories you want to use, for magic or trade, by recounting them.

## Light and fading

Only beads light the Dreamlands; held memories light only your Temenos. A bead sheds its memory's [[memory light]] in its emotions' colour. Covering a bead hides its light but does not slow its fading. In a bead, a memory fades at a rate set by the glass quality, and the glass also changes how fast a memory flows in or out.

## Condition

A bead has no [[Hold]]: a memory recounted into one loses its Hold, and comes back Loose when relived. A bead's condition is the vividness its memory has left and the quality of its glass. Use leaves no mark (see [[resonance]]).

## Cracking

In a bead, a memory cracks from low vividness and poor glass, over time and from blows. Conjuring with too much power can also crack a bead. Where opposing light meets in the Dreamlands it sparks into [[lucidity]], so a faint, Loose bead may crack. A cracked memory spills out as [[ambient emotion]] where it broke. See [[Memory light#Cracking|cracking]].

## Beads as goods

Personal beads are valuable goods priced in obols, and selling one means you can no longer conjure it. What sets a bead's value, and how worn beads are passed off as fresh, is covered under [[Economy]], where several of these points are still open.

## Other uses

- [[Planting]] a memory gives the bead to a place, which takes it in.
- An [[illusion]] used as a [[guise]] is a conjuration from a bead holding a memory of the person.
- On the [[meeting ground]], no bead is involved: memories move directly between the two houses.
