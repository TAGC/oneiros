An **anchored place** is a place you can fast-travel to from your [[Temenos]], through its door in the hallway. The hallway holds doors to anchored places.

[[Planting]] a memory in a place is one way to anchor it: planting anchors fast-travel doors.

[[Stability]], how much an entity's form shifts with its feelings, is high for anchored places and old beings.

> [!OPEN]
> Planting is one way to anchor a place; other ways are open. [[Crafting]] lists anchors, but what they do is not yet set.
