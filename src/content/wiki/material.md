**Material** is [[force]] in a particular motion (an [[emotion]] or [[Calm]]), in the same units spells use.

Every [[memory]] and [[belief]] is a form plus amounts of material per emotion. Colourless ones hold Calm.

## What it sets

A memory's or belief's [[strength]] has two halves: how much (its material) and how rooted ([[Hold]] for a memory, [[Conviction]] for a belief). [[Strength]] owns that rule.

- **[[Mood capacity]]:** each emotion's mood capacity is that emotion's material across everything you hold, whatever the tier.
- **[[Coherence]]:** material times a tier factor sets each memory's or belief's share of maximum Coherence (see [[Coherence#Maximum Coherence|maximum Coherence]]).

Only a belief's own material counts towards mood capacity and Coherence, never its [[weight]], so nothing is counted twice.

## In use

You [[Resonance|conjure from a bead]] by feeding it material in the memory's own ratio. [[Burning]] a memory pours its material straight into your [[mood]]. A memory's light is its material running down (see [[Memory light]]).
