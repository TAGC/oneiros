**Raw magic** is the source of magic that moves: it shifts the [[mood]] of existing things, and the thing acts it out. Raw magic never conjures. You pay with your own mood.

A fire given Despair sinks and dies; a door filled with Fear flinches open; a seed given Hope lifts and grows.

Its [[composition]] is the [[chain]] you build (see [[Casting]]). It is one of three sources, compared on [[Magic#Three sources|Magic]].

## Force

A Raw spell carries your [[force]], and when it lands it grows the target's circle and pinches it its way, unlike a [[stimulus]], which moves none (see [[Stimulus#Stimulus and spells|Stimulus]]).

## Cost

Casting with the [[grain]] lasts; against it, the target's mood drifts back. The real cost comes from the target's size and [[stability]], its grain, the [[adverb]] and how far the spell is from your mood (see [[Casting#Cost and preview|Casting]]).

## Other uses

Raw or Memory magic, cast in the Dreamlands on a sleeping body, can open a mind to reach its [[meeting ground]].
