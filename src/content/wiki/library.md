The **library** rings the upper floor of your [[Temenos]]'s centre: an annular gallery looking down through the light onto the [[apeiron pool]]. It is the colourless counterpart of the wings.

## What it holds

- **Colourless beliefs**, as codices.
- **Colourless memories**, as white-flamed lamps.
- **Its own reading-room desk**, for colourless [[Belief stages|Musings]]. Coloured Musings sit on their own floor's reading-room desk (see [[Temenos#Wings and floors]]).
- **[[Thread|Threads]]** (quests), tied to the [[Belief|beliefs]] they hang off. Every version of a [[rumour]] you hear becomes a belief at Notion, and your library keeps the threads hanging off them.

## Deductions and insights

Place beliefs side by side to find [[Deduction|deductions]], which become new beliefs. Sometimes you wake with an [[insight]].

## Getting there

The library's doors open onto the four wings' upper floors. The cellars are reached only from below, past the pool.

## At zero Coherence

When you return as an [[Echo]], you keep your library and what you knew: your colourless beliefs and memories. Whether an Echo keeps more is open (see [[Echo]]).

The [[spinning top]] may move here from beside the pool; that is open (see [[Spinning top]]).
