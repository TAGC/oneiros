**Core and persona** are the two equipment layers. Equipment uses body-part slots with a **core** layer, which affects you, and a **persona** layer, which is how others see you.

## Core self

Inside your [[Temenos]], you appear as your core self.

## Persona gear

Persona gear can support a [[guise]] in the Dreamlands, of either kind: an illusion, or a change of your own form.

## Gear

Gear has [[stability]] too, so it reshapes, slips off or binds the change when you [[Flicker|flicker]] (see [[Stability#Gear]]). Gear can amplify, protect, widen or store, using the shared effects grammar; the designer has not confirmed that list (see [[Crafting]]).

Among the vision's [[Inspirations|inspirations]], Dwarf Fortress gives layered body parts and clothing.
