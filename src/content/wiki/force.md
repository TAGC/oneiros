**Force** (Kraft, Mainländer's term) is the amount of a will's motion: the area of its [[mood]]'s circle. Each will has its own force, and the world's force is the sum of theirs.

[[Emotion|Emotions]] are force's motions, and [[Calm]] is force circling at rest. [[Material]] is force in a particular motion, and [[Memory|memories]] and [[Belief|beliefs]] store it in a shape.

## Conserved

Force is conserved: never created, and lost only when opposed [[Held and manifest|manifest]] motions annihilate, which is how the world runs down. The law is named [[Conservation of force|conservation of force]].

When a form that holds force dissolves, the force is not lost. It hangs where it happened as [[ambient emotion]].

## Gaining and losing it

A will's total force is capped by its maximum [[Coherence]]. A will regains force mainly by eating: every living thing exists at the expense of others.

A [[stimulus]] changes the shape of a mood without moving any force. A [[Raw magic|Raw]] spell carries your force, and casting spends area from the lobe of the emotion you spend, so the pinch and your total both shrink. See [[Stimulus]] for the difference.

## Force from a spell

Force from a spell that lands is the target's to spend freely: what you send, they carry. If the target has no capacity for that emotion, all of it is excess, so beings made of one feeling are fragile to any other.

## Across the world

The world's force diminishes over aeons, too slowly for most inhabitants to notice. Along [[Seam|seams]], neighbouring [[Shard|shards]]' opposed ambient emotion annihilates; with [[Forgetting]], this is how the world's force wears down. See [[Cosmology]].
