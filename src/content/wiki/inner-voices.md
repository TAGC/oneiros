**Inner voices** are your emotions speaking. Emotions are the only regular voices. They speak in the event log, one line per beat, each in its own rhythm and louder as you lean into it: Fear stammers, Hate clips its words. [[Memory magic|Memory]] speaks in recognition; [[Unmaking]] whispers and tempts.

## What a voice wants

What a voice wants comes from the [[Quality of the will|quality]] feeding it, and that holds for the whole scene. Hate fed by Envy says "She has what should be yours"; fed by Injustice, "They deserve worse than this".

Your [[Belief|beliefs]] shape what they argue for. Fear with the belief _red things are faster_ says "Paint the cart red. We can outrun them."

## Voices suggest

Your strongest feeling's inner voice points out what it could do here: Hate notices the wall your enemies lean on. The voices serve the feeling, not you, and following one deepens the quality behind its line.

## When a quality finds its own voice

At its greatest depth a quality finds its own voice. It arrives with its unique feature in the house (see [[Quality styles]]) and a one-time log note. It may then speak under its own name once per scene, recognising its own shape rather than urging, and can propose a [[Belief stages|Musing]].

When a quality's opposite deepens to nearly match it, the two emotions they feed argue: Hope through Greed against Despair through Obduracy.

## Quiet

Empty of feeling, you go quiet and hollow. The Calm qualities never urge: after a long quiet, Humility or Temperance may offer one unnamed line of restraint, and Holiness speaks once in [[Stillness]].

## Elsewhere

- Musings come from, among other things, your Voices (see [[Belief stages]]).
- The Ache [[scar]] malus is that emotion's inner voice speaking of the loss and tugging your mood.

## Writing the lines

Lines are written in two parts, what each quality wants and how each emotion sounds, with rhythm partly applied by rule.
