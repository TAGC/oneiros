You are **Ablaze** while a [[memory]] [[Burning|burns]]. The burning memory shows as an orb coloured by its emotions, its rim showing the time left.

## How long it lasts

Time is world-time, and how long you stay Ablaze scales with the memory's [[vividness]].

## While Ablaze

Spells using the memory's emotions take on its sensory details. The memory also shows effects it would have at a higher tier: a [[flicker]], its [[waking boon]] and, for vivid enough memories, its [[eidolon]], the brief form of its [[mutation]], drawback in full.

## When it ends

The burn ends when its time runs out, when you have spent its material of each of its emotions, or when burning another memory replaces it. Then anything above your [[mood capacity]] escapes through your body as an [[outburst]].

Burnt material still within mood capacity stays in your [[mood]], and [[dwelling]] can later soak it into [[Belief|beliefs]].
