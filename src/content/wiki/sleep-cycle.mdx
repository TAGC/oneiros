The **sleep cycle** is the world's version of day and night. The [[Dreamlands]] turn through **Light, Deep and Vivid**, each cycle lasting about 30 to 45 minutes of play.

## Light

The longest phase, about 40% of the cycle: Calm, a time for travel and trade.

## Deep

The middle phase, about 35%: still and heavy, and the deep layers come closer. See [[Deep]].

## Vivid

The shortest and most dangerous phase, about 25%: the world shifts and Nightmares come out. Frightened villagers call it "the Lurid".

- [[Forgetting]] surges during the Vivid.
- Cities hang [[Obol|obols]] as street lamps through the Vivid, so a city's coin keeps it from shifting.
- [[Dreamstorm|Dreamstorms]], rare and violent, strike in the Vivid and reshape whole places.
- What starts a [[rumour]] often happens in the Vivid, and if enough people believe a version, the dream makes it real during the Vivid.

## People react

Shops close before the Vivid, factions shelter or celebrate, a night market opens, and afterwards everyone gossips about what changed.

## Measured in cycles

Several rules run on the sleep cycle:

- **[[Attention]]** always leaks away over a sleep cycle.
- **[[Wonder]]** fades Attention flares once per [[marvel]] per cycle.
- **[[Illusion|Illusions]]** fade by the next sleep cycle.
- **[[Apeiron]]** that is spilt or stolen returns to the pool after a sleep cycle.
- **A long [[rest]]** is sleep through a full sleep cycle.

## In the house

A house's phases mirror the world's. During a long rest, in the Deep recent memories consolidate, their [[Hold]] rising, and in the Vivid weak memories are most likely to be lost and the house reshuffles (see [[Rest]]). The phases also decide when it is best to enter another character's mind; [[Other minds]] has the full table.
