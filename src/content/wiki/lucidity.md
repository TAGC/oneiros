**Lucidity** is what opposites annihilate into. It counters the dream itself: it dissolves forms in proportion to how weakly they are held, and draws the [[Dreamer]]'s [[Attention]]. Its colour is a brilliant white flash.

Opposites annihilate only where both are [[Held and manifest|manifest]]; held feeling never does. [[Held and manifest]] owns that rule.

## Where it comes from

Wherever opposites meet, they annihilate into lucidity:

- **In a [[chain]]:** opposites cancel pair by pair, and each cancelled pair releases lucidity. Hate 4 and Love 2 make Hate 2 and lucidity 2.
- **In [[Adverb|adverbs]]:** a verb's opposite as adverb cancels into a lucidity dispel, and opposite adverbs cancel into lucidity.
- **Between clashing spells**, such as Love against Hate.
- **Against manifest feeling:** a Joy spell striking Grief armour bursts part of it into sparks of lucidity.
- **In a held [[undertone]]:** cancelling it releases lucidity.
- **Between opposed creatures.**
- **Where opposing light meets:** opposing [[Memory light|memory light]] in the Dreamlands sparks into lucidity, which grazes weakly held things, so a faint, Loose [[bead]] may crack. Calm light shelters with no sparks.
- **Where sibling [[Shard|shards]] of opposite feeling meet,** so [[Seam|seams]] shimmer with lucidity.

How held undertones and opposed creatures fit the held and manifest rule is open (see [[Held and manifest#Open questions]]).

## What it does

Lucidity dissolves forms in proportion to how weakly they are held: by [[Coherence]], [[stability]] and being [[Remembered]]. [[Illusion|Illusions]] go first, and it barely grazes the strongly held, you included.

What it dissolves releases its [[force]] as [[ambient emotion]], which can cancel into more lucidity, so cascades are possible.

It also reveals [[Ring|rings]]: you see them when you see clearly through lucidity or [[Wonder]]. It is capped per cast.

Your [[spinning top]] steadies your form, so lucidity and [[Forgetting]] wear on you less.

## Attention

Lucidity draws the Dreamer's [[Attention]], like glimmer in Caves of Qud. Attention owns how it rises and leaks.
