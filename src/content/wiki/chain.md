A **chain** is the run of [[emotion|emotions]] built on the wheel when you cast. Repeats are [[intensity]]: `Hate Hate` is Hate at intensity 2. Opposites in a chain cancel pair by pair into [[lucidity]] (see [[Casting#Composition|Casting]]).

A chain is only a plan until you cast it, and its opposites cancel at the cast (see [[Held and manifest]]). [[Raw magic]] takes its [[composition]] from the chain you build.

## Chain cap

A chain runs up to a **chain cap**. The cap is shared between verbs and adverbs, so you trade intensity for shaping (see [[Casting#Chained adverbs|chained adverbs]]).
